## Summary

Ixia is described as a beautiful place and a popular tourism destination.

The US government is sending aid to Ixia as part of their military campaign, drawing opposition from the [Friends of Humanity](Friends of Humanity.md). The Ixian War is a divisive political topic.

- Apollo is referred to as The Prince of Ixia or Crown Prince of North Ixia, though he finds the title inaccurate. He also goes by [Dauphin](wikipedia_Dauphin_of_France.md), a French title meaning "heir apparent". His father Mikhail is referred to as a warlord, and has the authority equivalent to a governor and a general. Apollo wants to represent Ixia in the Olympics, which they weren't invited for.

Apollo's family is hated by other Ixians, according to - The Doorman.

- Kelvin has done an expedition to Ixia.

- Grey Talon has visited South Ixia.

1. South Ixia South Ixia is the 52nd state of the [United States](United States of America.md). The anniversary of its annexation is celebrated across the country. The Ixian War has not yet reached South Ixia.

1. North Ixia North Ixia is a sovereign region described as "a land of beauty, tradition and conflict". It is plagued by political violence, which forced Apollo to move to [New York](New York.md). [Fairfax Industries](Fairfax Industries.md) is selling weapons to North Ixia. Eldtritch Tactical Solutions, a subsidiary of Fairfax Industries, is operating in North Ixia.

## Ixians

Ixians are beings native to Ixia, colloquially called demons by some, a term that - Apollo finds offensive. After the first [Maelstrom](Maelstrom.md), some Ixians have immigrated to Earth, leading to a new population of Earth-born Ixians.

- Infernus and the daughter-in-law of - Grey Talon are some of them.

Ixians are characterized by a demon-like appearance with horns and a tail (with the exception of Infernus) and a variety of skin colors including red, blue and black. Some of them have a supernatural control of fire, manifested in Infernus and in Grey Talon's daughter-in-law.

Infernus has never visited Ixia, as he was born and raised in [New York](The Cursed Apple.md).

- Abrams is surprised to learn this, implying that he has visited Ixia. He is also fluent in Ixian. Infernus doesn't speak Ixian nor is familiar with the language, as his parents only speak English around him. He believes they moved to New York because they were unhappy in Ixia. Apollo is surprised that Infernus does not recognize him.

- Cadence is a popular Ixian singer.

It seems to be possible for Ixians to have children with humans. It's currently unknown if these "half-Ixians" inherit all the Ixian traits from their parent.

Apollo uses "gods" as an interjection, indicating that Ixians may have a [polytheistic](wikipedia_Polytheism.md) religion.

## Language

Ixia has its own language. As a native Ixian, Apollo is fluent.

- Abrams is also fluent.

- Rem, Seven and - Grey Talon use Ixian phrases.

- Infernus doesn't speak Ixian. One of the whispered voice lines for - Cultist Sacrifice seems to speak in Ixian.
