The Golden Statues (Sometimes also referred to as Golden Urn) are breakables found across the map. You can break these by shooting, punching, dashing, sliding or even using various abilities. Upon breaking, they have a chance to drop a permanent buff. They do not drop M Souls like the [crates](crates.md) do.

## Granted Buffs

Upon breaking a Golden Statue, there is a 50% chance that it drops a permanent stat buff. You can pick up/acquire the buff once walking over the bottle that drops. After the 10 minute mark, the buffs slightly increase in value into a second tier, at 30 minutes, they are buffed to the third tier.
