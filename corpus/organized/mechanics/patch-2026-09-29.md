A massive visual update to the map and neutrals, six new heroes, a mysterious figure known as The Broker, HUD updates, numerous quality-of-life additions, accessibility options, and more.

## Heroes

Added voting for six new heroes, H Nurse Harrow, H Deadman Danny, H Baba, H Solomon, H Rat King, and - H Violet. Two heroes release a week every Friday and Tuesday at 14:00, starting October 2nd.

## Map

Major Map reworks, making each lane more unique and adding four neutral sectors.

Steam Vents have been added that provide invisibility and slight health regeneration when stood on.

Added new neutral enemies (though in, primarily, the same positions).

Neutrals have been renamed to Haunts.

Added Tough Crates, crates that require a heavy melee to open but which always drop more souls than normal crates.

Added Healing Snacks, hidden around the map, that heal 10% of Max Health per snack.

Golden Statues have been renamed to Buff Containers.

Buff Containers and Sinner's Sacrifices now also provide Spirit Resist, Bullet Resist, Ability Range and Movement Speed as buffs.

Sinner's Sacrifices now have cursed version, which provide an extra buff but scroll through their lights twice as fast.

Added The Broker, a temporary NPC who will be removed when the first new Hero releases.

He spawns at 30 minutes and allows you to Corrupt an item, enhancing it with a randomized drawback.

## Other

Unsecured souls are now Haunted souls.

Parrying a Guardian no longer resets your parry cooldown.

Street Brawl gives you the chance to Corrupt an item in Round 5.

Shop UI updated.

Killfeed UI updated.

Dialogue Viewer added to the hideout.

Performance improvements, anti-cheat improvements, and engine updates.

New SFX for various actions.

Melee SFX have been replaced.

A massive number of bug fixes and miscellaneous changes.
