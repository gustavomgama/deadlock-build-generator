Cosmic Veils are visual barriers located around the [Map](Map.md) at all [Juke Rooms](The Cursed Apple.md#Juke-Rooms) and beneath most structural arches. They do not block movement, but slightly obscure vision beyond the barrier acting like one-way window film. Depending on the side, the barrier will either be of a gray colorless filter or rainbow shine filter.

On the gray colorless side, it will block out any perception of color and more importantly hide all enemy heroes as well as abilities performed by any player from the opposite side. Several visual effects will be visible but most player created game objects cannot be seen through the gray side.

Meanwhile, the rainbow shine side will allow players to see others on the other side of the cosmic veil. As such, this game mechanic is often used as a means in [positioning](Strategy.md#Positioning) to attack enemies with surprise or escaping from a chase.
