Items are in-game equipment that provide [Heroes](Heroes.md) with bonus stats and special abilities.

## Overview

There are currently items available for purchase from any [Curiosity Shop](The Curiosity Shop.md) on the [map](The Cursed Apple.md), as well as - Street Brawl additional "Legendary" items available only in [Street Brawl](Street Brawl.md). Items in Street Brawl also have a chance to be "Enhanced," increasing their power (Enhanced stats can be found in each item's page).

Heroes start a standard match with nine item slots in their inventory, which can hold an item of any type or cost. Additionally, there are three [Extra Slots](Extra Slots.md) that unlock after destroying enemy [Walker](Walker.md)s.

Items are split into four tiers based on their price; all items within a tier have the same price. Some Tier 2+ items have a "component item" from a previous tier, and the component item will be consumed ("upgraded") to provide a discount to the new purchased item equal to the component's cost.

Example: Extra Spirit is a Tier 1 item (Cost:

- 800 ) that is a component for both Improved Spirit & Surge of Power. When either item is purchased, Extra Spirit will be consumed and the cost of the new item will be discounted by - 800.

Purchased items can be sold back to the shop, refunding half of their cost. Items can be refunded for their full value if the item is sold before leaving the Curiosity Shop area (highlighted as a yellow rectangle on the ground).

## Item Types

There are three types of items: Weapon: Improves base weapons, damage, and bullet on-hit effects.

Spirit: Improves abilities and spirit power.

Vitality: Improves survivability, defense, and movement.

1. Investment bonuses Purchasing items provide corresponding stats bonuses known as "Investments." Investment bonuses increase in strength based on the total value of items owned per type, up to - 28,800 for each type.

Example: Purchasing Extended Magazine (

- 800 ) as the first Weapon item will grant the first bonus of +9% weapon damage. Buying a Tier 2 Weapon item such as - Stalker (

- 1,600 ) increases the total soul investment of Weapon items to - 2,400, granting an additional +3%, accumulating to a total of +15% weapon damage. The investment is lost if the item is sold.

At - 4,800, a significant investment bonus is gained. This is commonly called the 4.8k spike.

## Active Items

Active items provide additional, temporary effects when activated by a key press. They have round corners in the Item shop. Upon being purchased, they must be bound to one of four individual keys for activation. As a result, no more than four active items can be owned at a time.

The assigned slot for an active item can be changed by holding Tab or Alt and then clicking and dragging the item between slots.

## Cosmetic Items

Cosmetic items are a unique type of active item that are only available during [Seasonal events](Seasonal events.md) and specific periods. These items are globally unlocked, don't take an item slot or provide bonus stats, and use a unique active item slot ("E" key).

These items may be used outside their events by using the "

- giveitem " [console command](Console commands.md) in the [Hideout](Hideout.md), Sandbox, or cheat-enabled custom games.
