- Infernus riding the Broadway zipline.](Riding_zipline.png) The Ziplines, also colloquially known as Transit Lines or Hyperlines are the primary method of long-distance transportation in [Deadlock](Deadlock.md). They run from each team's base down the three lanes, with availability depending on how far a team's [Troopers](Troopers.md) are currently pushed.

## Basic Mechanics

Travel speed on Ziplines is fixed at 18 m/s on the inner lane and 21 m/s on the outer lanes, independent of a hero's base speed. Using a Zipline refreshes the hero's air dash and double jump. Pressing the down key (Ctrl by default) drops the hero from the Zipline while preserving momentum for 2 seconds.

Players start at their lane's zipline at the beginning of the match. Switching lanes will move them to the corresponding zipline. At the team's base, the player's starting lane zipline is highlighted with a halo effect.

## Zipline Speed Boost & Express

The 'Zipline Speed Boost' (F by default) can be activated while riding to grant the 'Zipline Boosted' buff. This ability has the following properties: Speed Increase: +80% travel speed Duration: 32 seconds Cooldown: 360 seconds (6 minutes) Initial Unlock Time: 360 seconds (6 minutes) into a match Destroying a [base guardian](Guardian.md#Base-Guardian) permanently applies this 80% boost to its corresponding Zipline, turning it into an 'Express Line'.

Additionally, the Movement [Powerup](Powerup.md) increases Zipline Speed by 40-80% (scaling with game time) when active. This applies to both regular speed and boost.

## Combat Interactions

1. Usage Lockout Taking damage from an enemy hero or enemy objective prevents players from attaching to a Zipline for 3 seconds. This includes external damage sources from enemy players, such as a [Trooper](Trooper.md) with Lil Helpers from Rem or a McGinnis Mini Turret. Taking damage from neutral objectives such as [Denizens](Denizens.md), [Sinner's Sacrifice](Sinner's Sacrifice.md), or enemy troopers (including enemy Rift Troopers) does not prevent a player from attaching to a Zipline.

1. Getting Knocked Off If a hero takes damage from an enemy hero while already riding or attaching to a Zipline, they will be knocked off. This has several effects: Damage: 15% of max health (shown as 'Hyperline' in the incoming damage overview; reducible by [Spirit Resist](Spirit Resist.md)).

Stun: 2.5 seconds. The stun timer does not begin until the hero lands on the ground.

Fall: The hero falls directly down from the Zipline.

After riding a Zipline for three consecutive seconds, a blue particle effect appears, indicating immunity to being knocked off. However, while immune to being Knocked Off, damage taken will apply an additional 53.6% of damage as Hyperline damage; reducible by [Spirit Resist](Spirit Resist.md).

Be aware, as certain damage over time abilities will not knock a hero off a Zipline. For example, [Shiv](Shiv.md)'s Serrated Knives, Mirage's Djinn's Mark, and Infernus Afterburn will not knock a hero off after the initial damage is dealt.
