A Unit is an entity in [Deadlock](Deadlock.md) that possesses stats and can perform tasks.

## Heroes

[Heroes](Heroes.md) are player-controlled Units belonging to each team that have various abilities and can level up.

## Creeps

Creeps are Units that respawn, either in waves or after a specific time. Creeps can belong to a team or be neutral.

- Trooper

- l1: Troopers [Haunts](Haunt.md) Midboss

## Structures

Structures are large Units belonging to each team. They do not respawn.

[Lane Guardians](Lane Guardian.md) Walker

- l1: Walkers [Base Guardians](Base Guardian.md) Shrine

- l1: Shrines Patron

- l1: Patrons

## Minions

Minions are Units created by [Heroes](Hero.md).

- Graves ' Borrowed Decree

- l1: Ghouls McGinnis ' Mini Turret

- l1: Mini Turrets Rem's Lil Helpers

## Other

[Base Turrets](Base Turrets.md) are unique indestructible units.
