Barriers are a temporary pool of additional health that takes damage before [Health](Health.md) is reduced. Barriers block all incoming damage from any source, with [Damage Resistance](Damage Resistance.md)s applied.

## Notes

Damage to barriers is affected by [Damage Reduction](Damage Reduction.md) and [Damage Amplification](Damage Amplification.md).

Any instance of excess damage dealt to completely destroy a barrier is then done to health. For example, a Dynamo with 100 barrier health and 0% [Spirit Resist](Spirit Resist.md) takes 168 spirit damage from Vindicta's Assassinate. 100 spirit damage will break Dynamo's barrier, the remaining 68 spirit damage is done to health.

Healing amplification and [Healing Reduction](Healing Reduction.md) effects have no effects on newly created barriers. The only thing that can affect value of a new barrier is Rem's Tag Along.

Bullets with Armor Piercing Rounds can ignore barriers.

Self damage ignores barriers.
