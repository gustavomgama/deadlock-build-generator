Status effects are buffs/debuffs applied to you that change properties of either you or your enemy. These usually buff attributes or reduce attributes (e.g. dash distance).

Below are a list of all the movement relevant status effects in the games: [Movement Slow](Movement Slow.md) [Stun](Stun.md) [Displace](Displace.md) [Immobilize](Immobilize.md) [Purge](Purge.md) Character-specific Status Effects: [Petrify](Petrify.md) [Tether](Tether.md) [Time Stop](Time Stop.md) [Sleep](Sleep.md) [Hex](Hex.md)
