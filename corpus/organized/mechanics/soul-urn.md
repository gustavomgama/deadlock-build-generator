- + The Soul Urn, also known as Spirit Urn, Soul Jar, Spirit Jar or Idol, is an objective that spawns at the -minute mark and respawns in -minute intervals.

Players can pick up the urn and deliver it to its destination at the side lanes of the map. Doing so grants the courier [Souls](Souls.md) and 4 [Golden statue](Buff Container.md) buffs.

## Spawn

The soul urn spawns next to the bridge, alternating between Yellow and Green Lane. The first urn will always spawn in Yellow.

The urn respawns and alternates lanes every minutes. It descends from the sky for 12.5 seconds before it can be picked up. A player can grab the landed urn by hitting it with a [Melee Attack](Melee Attack.md) (light or heavy).

The drop-off point is on the opposite side of the map to the urn spawn. If the urn hasn't been picked up in 3 minutes, it will start walking on its own to the drop-off via the [Mid-Boss](Mid-Boss.md) stairs, making up to about halfway through before it respawns on the other side. It can still be picked up while it's walking. If it is picked up before the next respawn time, the next respawn is delayed until the urn is either delivered or expires.

## Carrying the Urn

While carrying the urn, the courier is affected in several ways: They get a bonus of +2 m/s [Move Speed](Move Speed.md) (capped at 15 m/s max), +1 [Stamina](Stamina.md), +10% Dash Distance, +25% Stamina Regen, and have 100% [Slow Resistance](Slow Resistance.md).

If they are on the trailing team, the courier also gets bonus [Bullet](Damage Resistance.md), [Spirit](Damage Resistance.md), and [Debuff Resist](Debuff Resist.md), plus +5 m/s Sprint Speed. These scale with how far behind the team is in souls, reaching maximum at a `*100`% net worth difference.

## Reward

The Soul Urn's base soul bounty is Souls and increases by Souls per minute of game time. This value is "locked in" at the minute the Urn spawns.

The souls are released as [Soul Orbs](Soul Orbs.md) and can be denied. If not denied, all souls go to the courier. If an ally secures an orb, they share the orb value with the courier.
