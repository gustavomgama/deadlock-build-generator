Tunnels are a network of shortcut passages in the [map](map.md) connecting different locations. Some tunnels are only accessible by vents, which only certain heroes can fit through. [Crates](Crates.md) and [Buff Container](Buff Container.md)s also spawn within vent tunnels.

## Overview

Tunnel vents can only be used by specific characters, and may not be used while in combat (taking or dealing damage within a 4s buffer time, although taking [barrier](barrier.md) damage doesn't count) or while carrying the - name: Soul Urn.

The vent entrances have a grill as a visual indicator to players within 10 metres if these are inaccessible at the given moment.

The following heroes can access vents: Calico in - Ava form.

- Mo & Krill by pressing "crouch" next to the vent (CTRL by default) or during Burrow.

- Rat King by pressing "crouch" next to the vent.

- Rem when crouching.

1. Notes Grey Talon's Guided Owl can also enter vents, if the player manages to enter at the correct angle without hitting any walls. It can also enter and exit the tunnels through the gap over the fence in the subway room next to [Mid-Boss](Mid-Boss.md).

While Mo & Krill and - Rat King can enter vents by crouching, there is a short delay when doing so. They also cannot enter vents by sliding.

The [Street Brawl](Street Brawl.md) item Shrink Ray allows any hero to enter vents. However, this has limited usability since Street Brawl takes place in one lane.

While in the tunnels, characters gain a 70% boost to their [Move Speed](Move Speed.md). Sprint speed is not multiplied by the tunnel boost.

If a hero requires an ability to enter vents and the ability duration runs out inside a vent or tunnel too small to walk/crouch on, the effect is different depending on the hero: For - Calico, she can activate the transformation and be pushed to the nearest walkable area. If she doesn't transform, she will stay as Ava and start rapidly taking damage, and transform back when she exits the space.

For - Mo & Krill, they will instantly exit Burrow in one of the nearest walkable spaces either inside or outside the tunnels.

[Crates](Crates.md) and [Buff Container](Buff Container.md)s can be found inside tunnels, and cockroaches that can be killed for - 1.

## Known Bugs

A player without vent-accessing abilities may accidentally get stuck in the tunnels by glitching through geometry. In this case, they can use the "Unstick" button in the main menu.
