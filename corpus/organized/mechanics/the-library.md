The Library was an unfinished [Deadlock](Deadlock.md) feature. The Library contained visual novels that would expand the world and lore of Deadlock through interactive, branching narratives.

It was planned that cosmetic items may be unlocked as rewards for completing the visual novel paths.

The Library used to be accessible in-game in an earlier build, but has since been removed.

## Summary

1. The Binding of Oathkeeper The Binding of Oathkeeper [Lady Geist](Lady Geist.md) has to find a way to deal with [Oathkeeper](Oathkeeper.md).

1. Infernus' visual novel [Infernus](Infernus.md) has to save the bar he works at, Jezebel's, from being closed.

1. Ivy's visual novel Ivy's visual novel [Ivy](Ivy.md) wants to get a job to help [the Arroyos](Minor characters.md).

1. Holliday's visual novel Holliday's visual novel [Holliday](Holliday.md) arrives in [New York](Cursed Apple.md) on the hunt for [the Troubadour](Minor characters.md).
