Ivy's visual novel is an upcoming [Deadlock](Deadlock.md) [visual novel](Visual Novels.md).

The novel is accessible in-game through the [console command](Console commands.md) citadel_book_open 3, but only the audio is available and it is currently incomplete. The audio available so far has been archived on [YouTube.](https://youtu.be/zDdlk9rz7Ro?si=nE4pJVTuUkvCDDPE) See also [Ivy's visual novel/Transcription](Ivy's visual novel_Transcription.md).
