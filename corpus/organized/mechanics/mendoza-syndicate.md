## Summary

18-20 years before the events of the ritual, the Mendoza Syndicate controlled Spanish Harlem. Members of the Syndicate harassed innocent people, including the [Arroyo family](The Arroyos.md). At this time, the gargoyle that would become known as - Ivy came to life and protected the Arroyos. Ivy was named and adopted by the family and vowed to dismantle the Mendoza Syndicate and free Spanish Harlem. She dedicated 18 years of her life to this mission.

After 18 years, Ivy succeeded in dismantling the Mendoza Syndicate, forcing Mendoza to leave Spanish Harlem. Mendoza himself told Ivy that he would leave New York after she took apart his criminal empire, however he's still implied to be operating elsewhere in New York without Ivy's knowledge. The [New York Oracle](The New York Oracle.md) asked local law enforcers if they believed the Mendoza Syndicate was truly gone.

Ivy says that she was able to take down the Mendoza Syndicate "without murdering everybody". The - name: Shopkeeper mentions that Ivy used to buy tier 4 [items](items.md) from him during her war against the Syndicate.

A long time ago, Infernus used to do work for the Mendoza Syndicate before he was replaced by - Vyper. Vyper herself killed a number of people for Mendoza before she was stopped by Ivy. After getting caught, Vyper was tried for 13 counts of murder, although she mentions that her actual kill count was higher.

It's currently unknown how much power Mendoza still has after his Syndicate was dismantled. One thing he still seems to do is bet and rig fights in the [Bear Pit](Bear Pit.md). He also has enough money or influence to send some goons to beat up Lash in [Ivy's visual novel](Ivy's visual novel.md). Mendoza used to be a client of - Mo & Krill, although Krill refers to him as a client in past tense.

While the Mendoza Syndicate has been dismantled, other crime lords have shown interest in taking over Spanish Harlem for themselves.

- Wraith was caught by Ivy looking around the neighbourhood and was warned to stay out.

- Yamato initially didn't care for Spanish Harlem, but considers taking it over for the [Seventh Moon](Seventh Moon.md) when Ivy mentions it to her. While on opposing teams to Ivy, Wraith and Yamato want to take over Spanish Harlem for Ivy's insubordination.

1. Mendoza The leader of the Mendoza Syndicate, Mendoza was a crime lord who was forced out of Spanish Harlem by Ivy's vigilante justice.

While Mendoza has not yet made an appearance in-game, several characters give impressions as to what Mendoza is like. Krill described Mendoza as a "good client", but doesn't deny him being a "bad man" as described by Ivy. Lash says Mendoza "says a lot of stupid shit", and mentions that the only person Mendoza hates more than him is Ivy, which Lash considers impressive. Yamato mentions that Mendoza fears Ivy.

One time, Mendoza told Wraith to bet money on Lash taking a dive in a fight, which Lash proceeded to win, causing Wraith to lose "a lot of money". Wraith herself dislikes Mendoza, and admires Ivy for "making Mendoza's life hell". Similarly, Mendoza may dislike Wraith as Vyper says "Mendoza says hello" when killing Wraith in the [ritual](Maelstrom.md).

It's suggested that Ivy may be worried that Mendoza would seek revenge against her by going after her family. The - name: Hidden King tells Ivy that it's only a matter of time before Mendoza goes after the Arroyos to hurt her. While Ivy is trapped in the Baroness Hotel, The Doorman taunts her by asking who would be protecting the Arroyos from Mendoza while Ivy is participating in the ritual.

While incomplete, the [leaked audio](https://youtu.be/zDdlk9rz7Ro?si=GtjdsierOR50xIXH&t=568) for [Ivy's visual novel](Ivy's visual novel.md) suggests that Mendoza will make an appearance in at least one of the pathways. In [Chapter 4e](Ivy's visual novel_Transcription.md#4e---Helping-Hand), Ivy and Lash fight some goons sent by Mendoza to beat up Lash, to which Ivy asks Lash if he can take her to Mendoza himself.
