## Overview

Fairfax Industries is a company in the occult industrial complex that specialises in spirit weaponry, headed by [Maximilian Fairfax](Maximilian Fairfax.md). As Maximilian's eldest heir, Pocket was in line to inherit the company before an attempt was made on their life. Fairfax Industries is described as an "avarice-fueled viper's nest" and "the beating heart of the Occult Industrial Complex". Old Fairfax factories can be found next to the - name: Hidden King's base in Yellow lane, and the Fairfax Plaza district can be found next to the Stock Exchange building in the - name: Archmother's side of Yellow lane.

They are currently under sanction by the Environmental Protection Agency for improperly disposing of spiritual waste.

Various Fairfax-branded [crates](crates.md) can be found across the [Cursed Apple](The Cursed Apple.md). Some stores have curtains with an "FF" logo.

Walter Fairfax, the father of Maximilian and the previous CEO of the company, died around 10 years after his son took over the company. Two statues of Walter Fairfax can be found near the Fairfax building in the [Plaza District](Plaza District.md) and behind the Theater, near the park on [Greenwich](Greenwich.md). He can also be seen in the background of Pocket, Apollo, and Ivy’s hero selection screen.

Fairfax Industries has a publishing arm called Fairfax Books.

- Dynamo carries one of their books in his bag.

Fairfax Industries has made a map of New York's subway lines, which can be seen in the subway tunnels.

1. Cadenza Fairfax Industries used to have sovereignty of the 50th state of the [US](United States of America.md), the island of Cadenza, which they are currently trying to regain. According to - McGinnis, Fairfax was calm during the "Cadenza situation", as he knew that legislation would eventually catch up to him. He's also proud for having created one of the United States. His employees at the time were "rattled", and McGinnis thought she would lose her job.

In the present, Cadenza still manufactures Fairfax products, and also holds corporate retreats for the company.

1. Eldritch Tactical Solutions Eldritch Tactical Solutions, or ETS, is a Paranormal Military Company and a subsidiary of Fairfax Industries. Its workers are known as "soldiers of fortune". The [New York Oracle](The New York Oracle.md) states that it has been accused of committing war crimes by previous employees. In a conversation with - Haze, Deadman Danny implies that Fairfax Industries downplays these accusations as "media slander" and that is a "fine company", but when she rebuttals with the fact she has operated in North Ixia, he ceases the conversation.

- Pocket wants to dismantle ETS.

- Deadman Danny offers positions in ETS to both Calico and - Haze, the latter replying she is not "psychotic enough" to work for them.
