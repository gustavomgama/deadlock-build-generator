## Stalker's Mark Teleport Momentum Conservation

- Teleport Momentum Conservation Stalker's Mark is - Drifter ’s second ability. Using it sends a mark, which upon hitting a valid target allows you to teleport to said target at any time during the duration of - Stalker's Mark.

To preserve [air dash](Air Dash.md) momentum, cast Stalker's Mark, air dash, then ambush while still holding air dash momentum. The air dash momentum you had before the teleport will be carried with you.
