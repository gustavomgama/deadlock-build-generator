Abilities do not level from Boons directly. Each level-up grants a Boon, and Boons grant either an **Ability Unlock**or an ** Ability Point**:

- Boon levels 0, 2, 4 and 7 grant an Ability Unlock.

- The first three unlocks can only be used on non-ultimate abilities; the ultimate unlocks at Boon level 7.

- Every other Boon level grants an Ability Point.

- Each ability has 3 upgrade tiers costing 1, 2, and then 5 Ability Points (8 points to fully max one ability).

## Build Notes

- One ability fully maxed: unlock + 8 Ability Points. With the unlock at Boon 0, that is reached at Boon level 11 (8 points).

- All four abilities fully maxed: 4 unlocks + 32 Ability Points, reached at Boon level 35.
