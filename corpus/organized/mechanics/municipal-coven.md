## Overview

The New York Municipal Coven work as the primary caretakers of New York. Their main priority is to keep the city clean, one of the notable examples being disposing of souls through Spirit Urns. The Municipal Coven oftentimes tasks "runners" to deliver the urns to designated delivery locations for a safe and speedy disposal.

It's assumed that the Coven primarily consists of witches or those who practice witchcraft, as a coven is a formal term for the structured gathering of witches.

The [Soul Urn](Soul Urn.md) is property, or rather a "worker", of the Municipal Coven.

During the festive season, the Municipal Coven were looking for volunteers to spend time with stray familiars in their Soho shelter.
