## Majestic Leap Cancel

During the descent from the air, you are able to conserve some momentum and go further by [air jumping](jumping.md). By doing this, you cancel [Majestic Leap](Majestic Leap.md). This is called a Majestic Leap Cancel.

This does not get rid of leap state.

Majestic Leap Cancelling allows you to travel further than a regular Majestic Leap. This can allow you to escape further or chase down an enemy player.

To execute, activate Majestic Leap, then descent. While descending, [jump](jump.md) to cancel Majestic Leap and maintain the momentum.
