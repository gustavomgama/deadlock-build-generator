## Overview

The Baroness Hotel is located on Broadway in the Cursed Apple, as well as being used for The Doorman's ultimate. The hotel is described by The Doorman as "the finest luxuries this plane can offer", although Lady Geist believes that the Baroness' standards "went to hell".

The luxuries of the Baroness include bottomless mimosas, an in-house tailor, Egyptian cotton sheets, a sauna, spa days and brunches.

The Doorman has worked at the Baroness since its inception.

- Mirage and [Nashala](Minor characters.md#Nashala-Dion) are currently guests at the Baroness. [Carlos](The Arroyo's.md#Carlos-Arroyo) and [Maria Arroyo](The Arroyo's.md#Maria-Arroyo) planned a date night at the hotel. [Ms. Shelly](Shelly Fisher.md) has a brunch scheduled at the Baroness.

The Baroness used to be known as "the most haunted hotel in America", but has lost the title after [the Troubadour](the Troubadour.md)'s actions in Macomb. Now it is only the most haunted hotel in the tri-state area.

At some point, the Baroness required the services of the [Baxter Society](The Baxter Society.md).

Other known workers of the Baroness are Jacques, and Francis.

## Gameplay

In-game, the Baroness can be found next to [the Archmother](The Archmother.md)'s blue [Guardian](Guardian.md). It has two [Denizen](Denizen.md) spawn rooms: A room with [3 small denizens](Denizen.md).

A room with [2 medium denizens](Denizen.md) and a [Sinners Sacrifice machine](Sinner's Sacrifice.md).

The Doorman's ultimate ability Hotel Guest sends an enemy to a supernatural hallway in the Baroness, which they need to escape from.
