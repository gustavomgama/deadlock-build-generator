List of known [movement tech](Character Tech.md) for Vindicta.

## Flight

Flight (by default, 2) is Vindicta's second ability, which launches her in the air and allows her to fly for - AbilityDuration.Value s.

Vindicta can [Sprint](Sprint.md) while flying.

Vindicta flies slower while aiming straight up or down during Flight. A visual indicator for this is when she begins to tilt her head to the side while moving the camera. She also moves slower to the left or right, but at her normal speed diagonally.

Vindicta flies slower up and down while moving horizontally.

If Vindicta's move speed is reduced below her base move speed from items such as - Sharpshooter and - Weighted Shots, she is more greatly affected by it. This can be counteracted by keeping her bonus move speed above zero with items like Enduring Speed or - Fortitude.

While flying, any speed above Vindicta's base move speed of - Vindicta MaxMoveSpeed m/s is halved. This limit can be increased with [Air Control](Gravity_Scale.md).

With - Magic Carpet's +25% air control, only speed above ` * 5/3 round 1` m/s is halved.

A friendly Holliday's Bounce Pad can indefinitely provide Vindicta with + Bounce Pad AirControlPercent % air control until she touches the ground.

- Majestic Leap also provides + Majestic Leap AirControlPercent % air control until Vindicta touches the ground. It must be activated prior to Flight, or else it will drag her down and force her to slide across the ground.

With 100% or more air control, Vindicta can fly much faster diagonally in certain directions.

Flying straight forward, Vindicta moves at her base walking speed of 9.5m/s. Without air control, she would fly at her usual halved speed of 8.7m/s.

Flying diagonally, Vindicta moves at 11.4m/s, which is 20% faster than if she flew forwards at 9.5m/s or 30% faster than if she flew at 8.7m/s with no air control at all.

Vindicta is fastest when she's moving back and to the left or forward and to the right while looking straight up. She flies at 13.4m/s, 40% faster than if she flew forwards at 9.5m/s, or 52% faster than if she flew at 8.7m/s without air control.

When aiming straight down, Vindicta is nearly as fast in those same directions, but only if she is very far above the ground. She moves slower in those directions the closer she is to the ground.

The other directions (forward and to the left, back and to the right) behave the opposite way. She moves slower in those directions the higher she is above the ground and faster close to the ground (while continuing to aim straight down).

Of all the diagonal directions, Vindicta is slowest when moving back and to the right regardless of height or aiming direction.

Vindicta cannot dash during the flight due to dash hotkey (SHIFT) being overridden by Fly Up. Rebinding either of those allows the player to dash normally during the flight.

Vindicta can [Air Strafe](Air Strafing.md) diagonally for additional speed while flying, which also benefits from air control. Unlike normal air strafing, she has to hold the forward movement key (W) to fly forward which limits her to air strafing diagonally.

When killing a target with Flight active and the T3 upgrade, flight is recast, giving Vindicta a burst of speed in the air but no additional height.

1. Super Jump

- name: Super Jump

- file: Vindictasuperjump.mp4

- tech_type: Character Tech

- difficulty: Advanced Easy

- inputs: (be moving) Flight → (Cancel flight) By cancelling Flight while you are being launched upwards, you can keep the momentum. Executing this is called a Super Jump. You can use the Super Jump forwards and backwards. Left and right don't work as well, and don’t go as far.

The Super Jump costs no bars of [stamina](stamina.md), but does deplete Flight.

The timing for a Super Jump can vary, and can lead to different results, but the most optimal timing is cancelling as just as the purple bar starts to deplete (or just before the bell rings and after the swoosh sound) gives maximum horizontal distance. This timing gives ~1600 units horizontal.

The Super Jump can be useful for chasing down an enemy opponent, moving around buildings quickly or to move quicker.

To execute, move in any direction, then activate Flight. When the purple bar starts to deplete, cancel Flight.

The Super Jump benefits from air control in all directions. With air control, it can be done faster diagonally following the same behavior as Flight. For example, Super Jumping forward and to the right or back and to the left while looking straight up will always give the most speed.

With the T3 upgrade, Vindicta can use the extra burst of speed from re-casting Flight on kills to Super Jump while flying. It is slightly slower than a normal Super Jump and does not add any vertical velocity.

1. Zipline Momentum Conservation

- name: Zipline Momentum Conservation

- file: vindictazmc.mp4

- tech_type: Character Tech

- difficulty: Advanced Easy

- inputs: (Be on transit line) → Duck (drop off the transit line) → Flight ZMC By using Zipline Momentum Conservation, you are able to move faster while in Flight.

ZMC can help you chase down an enemy or to retreat back into lane.
