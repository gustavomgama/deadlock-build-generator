- You're going to wish you had the Tunnel Rats on your side.

- Krill_unselect_03.mp3 The Tunnel Rats are an underground (both figuratively and literally) group that assists the black market that operates between the "boroughs", using a network of underground tunnels.

The Tunnel Rats used to do business with the [Mendoza Syndicate](The Mendoza Syndicate.md).

## Notable members

- Mo & Krill act as enforcers for The Tunnel Rats, punishing anyone who disrespects the group. They oppose the gangs who occasionally try and exploit the tunnel system.
