## Overview

Not much is known about Shadowline. It was presumably a transitional version from [Neon Prime](Neon Prime.md) to the Deadlock [Cursed Apple](The Cursed Apple.md) setting that existed in 2022-2023. The web domain playshadowline.com and twitter account @PlayShadowline were registered in November 2023.

In this version, the [ziplines](ziplines.md) were called "shadowlines", and [Astral Gates](Astral Gates.md) were "shadowline nexus". The shadowlines were presumably based on [ley lines](wikipedia_Ley_line.md).

## Mentions

The [Shopkeeper](Shopkeeper.md) narrated an old version of the tutorial that refers to the game as Shadowline.

[Kelvin](Kelvin.md) referenced a shadowline in one of his voice lines when upgrading his Ice Path. The voice line has since been removed.

In [Viscous'](Viscous.md) background, the Bermuda Triangle [Astral Gate](Astral Gates.md) is described as a "shadowline nexus".

## Caldera_trooper

Caldera_trooper was a [map](map.md) found in early builds of Deadlock that presumably started development during the Shadowline period. It had a similar layout to Neon Prime's map, but with new lighting and assets from what would later become the Cursed Apple. Caldera_trooper was last compiled in July 2023.
