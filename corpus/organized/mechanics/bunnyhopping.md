- tech_type: Universal Tech

- file: rawbunnyhop.mp4 Bunnyhopping is a movement technique used in video games where the player times jumps immediately upon landing to create a rhythmic hopping motion similar to a rabbit’s movement. The technique originated in early first-person shooters such as the [Quake series](wikipedia_Quake (series).md).

In Deadlock, bunnyhopping exists in two forms: [Slide Bunnyhopping](Slide Bunnyhopping.md) [Raw Bunnyhopping](Raw Bunnyhopping.md)
