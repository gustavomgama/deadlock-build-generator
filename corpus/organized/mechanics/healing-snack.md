- Vampirebat_use_power3_08.mp3 Healing Snack is a consumable found around [the Map](The Cursed Apple.md) that may be picked up by walking near them to regenerate [Health](Health.md).

## Overview

Healing Snacks spawn after 2 minutes and 30 seconds have elapsed in the match and respawn 3 minutes after being picked up. Healing Snacks stay in their respective location until they are picked up, restoring 10% of the hero's [Max Health](Max Health.md) over 4 seconds. The healing value is increased to 20% in Street Brawl.

## Locations

There are 36 Healing Snacks around the map, most alongside [Greenwich](The Cursed Apple.md#Greenwich) & [York](The Cursed Apple.md#York). Most are flying high in the air, meaning players must use some movement in order to reach them. They are not shown on the map, similar to [Crates](Crate.md) & [Buff Containers](Buff Container.md).
