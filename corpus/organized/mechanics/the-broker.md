The Broker was a shopkeeper on the [map](map.md) who allowed players to transform [Items](Items.md) into Corrupted Items. His shop was also accessed through round 5 on [Street Brawl](Street Brawl.md).

The Broker was available in Standard and [Ranked](Ranked mode.md) from [September 29, 2026](Update_September 29, 2026.md) to [October 2, 2026](Update_October 2, 2026.md), located in a corner on the right side of the Blue lane [Walker](Walker.md) for each team. He may return months later in a different location.

## Overview

When the Broker is available in Standard and [Ranked mode](Ranked mode.md), the shop first opens around ~30 minutes into a match, opening at 2 locations mirrored on both sides of the map. The shop reopens every ~15 minutes after it closes, allowing players to corrupt additional items.

## Corrupted Items

Corrupted items have many of their attributes adjusted, similar to enhanced items from [Street Brawl](Street Brawl.md), but with some random stat variance and an additional random downside with magnitude scaling with the item tier.
