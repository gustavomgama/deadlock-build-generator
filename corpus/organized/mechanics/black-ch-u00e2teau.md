The Black Château is a mysterious location in [Deadlock](Deadlock.md), frequented by academics.

## Summary

The Black Château's mysteries were revealed to the general public for the first time in a [New York Oracle](The New York Oracle.md) article.

- Dynamo and Kelvin last saw each other at the Black Château, before Kelvin's disappearance.

- Dynamo met one of Venator's associates, Father Julian, at the Black Château.

- Dynamo says "no one is leaving the Black Château" for Lady Geist's gala event.

The Soul Urn wants Dynamo to take it to the Black Château.
