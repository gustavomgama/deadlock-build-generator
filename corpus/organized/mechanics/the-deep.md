## Summary

Described as a shadowline nexus deep below the Atlantic Ocean, the Deep is an underwater habitat where an Astral Gate resides. As the Deep is deep underwater, the surface world doesn't interact with it much, making its culture significantly different from the surface.

- Viscous is a denizen of the Deep. The [Loch Ness Monster](Magical creatures.md#Loch-Ness-Monster) also came from the Deep's Astral Gate.

The Deep is being threatened by a "genocidal force of nature" known as The Adversary, who came through the Deep's Astral Gate. The denizens of the Deep are terrified of the Adversary, as they are currently getting "massacred" by the monster.

Viscous says the other denizens find legs "unnerving", but he enjoys having them.

- Calico has knowledge about the Deep.

The [OSIC](Occult Security and Investigation Commission.md) seems to worry about the creatures of the Deep.

1. The Adversary The Adversary is a powerful and dangerous being who came through the Deep's Astral Gate. Currently, the Adversary is massacring the denizens of the Deep, and Viscous believes that, if he can't stop the Adversary, it's only a matter of time before the Adversary starts killing everything on the surface.

Viscous has entered the ritual to stop the Adversary, hoping to get rid of it to save the Deep.

Most playable characters are unaware of what's happening in the Deep, and don't take the threat of the Adversary seriously.

- name: Billy refuses to believe in the Adversary unless he sees it with his own eyes. It's implied that the surface often ignores the troubles of the Deep.
