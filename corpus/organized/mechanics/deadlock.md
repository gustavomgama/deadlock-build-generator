The game is currently in a [closed beta](Early Development Build.md), access to which can only be provided by accepting a Steam Playtest [invite](Deadlock.md#Invites) requested by a Steam friend who already has access.

## Gameplay

Main article: [Mechanics](Mechanics.md) In Deadlock's standard mode, two teams of 6 players, The Hidden King and - The Archmother, battle through 3 lanes of [New York](New York.md). Each team fights on behalf of a - Patron. The objective is to destroy the enemy's Patron while defending your own to complete the ritual. Players control [Heroes](Heroes.md) with upgradable abilities, and items are bought using [souls](souls.md). Souls are earned by defeating enemies and completing tasks. They can also unlock 'boons' from Patrons, which boost Hero stats.

Heroes level up, unlock abilities, and equip [Weapon](Weapon.md), [Vitality](Vitality.md), and [Spirit](Spirit.md) items in slots. [Extra slots](Extra slots.md) are unlocked by destroying enemy Walker

- alt_name: Walkers.

The game blends elements of shooters and MOBAs. It focuses on tactics, teamwork, and strategy, with third-person skill shots, movement, and gunplay. It also has a unique [zipline](Ziplines.md) system for long-distance transportation.

In addition to the standard gamemode, Deadlock also offers a ranked mode for more competitive play, and a 4v4 roguelike gamemode called [Street Brawl](Street Brawl.md), where players fight in one lane over short rounds.

## Setting & Theme

Main article: [Lore](Lore.md) In the world of Deadlock, [occult](wikipedia_Occult.md) rituals and magic anomalies are part of everyday life. The game takes place in an alternate version of early 20th century New York called [The Cursed Apple](The Cursed Apple.md). Two covenants battle to complete the [Ritual](Ritual.md) and summon their [Patron](Patron.md), a god capable of granting wishes. Each character has their own wishes and reason for wanting to summon the Patron, and their fates collide on the streets of New York. The game's setting is an [urban fantasy](wikipedia_Urban_fantasy.md) with strong occult themes and elements of [noir](wikipedia_Noir_fiction.md), [gothic](wikipedia_Gothic_fiction.md), and [dieselpunk](wikipedia_Dieselpunk.md) fiction.
