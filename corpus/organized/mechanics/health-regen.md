Health Regen is a stat that [heroes](heroes.md) start with and can obtain more through [items](items.md). Per second, heroes will regenerate their amount of health regeneration as [health](health.md). It must not be confused with healing, which refers to the ability of helping both the player and other players regain health.

## Notes

As players take damage, their health will slowly recover over time. While [dead](Death.md), players will not be able to regenerate health. Standing in base will quickly regenerate the player's health (adding + Convar

- citadel_player_regen_zone_bonus_base health per second and + Convar

- citadel_player_regen_zone_bonus_pct % of max health per second), allowing them to recover from fights. Most sources of health regen are additive (health regen), with a few instead being multiplicative (Max health regen). Both types of regen do not increase by leveling up.
