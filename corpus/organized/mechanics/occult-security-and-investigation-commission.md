## Summary

The Occult Security and Investigation Commission works hard to ensure supernatural threats that are too big for local law enforcement to handle are taken care of. The OSIC keeps files on many notable persons, and once a threat to national security has been identified, a Sandman is sent to neutralize the threat.

The OSIC has warned [New York](New York.md) citizens to stay indoors for the duration of the [Maelstrom](Maelstrom.md).

- Haze has been sent to fight in the [ritual](Maelstrom.md) by the OSIC, although the details of her mission are currently unknown.

The OSIC wants

- name: Calico and - name: Seven dead. They seem to know that Seven is in New York, as Seven is hiding from them in New York's subway tunnels. The OSIC is also keeping an eye on - name: Wraith and - name: Yamato, although they haven't been considered worth allocating resources to take out yet. Haze mentions that if the [Seventh Moon](Seventh Moon.md) becomes a cartel, then her superiors would likely send her to kill Yamato. The OSIC doesn't seem to be after

- name: Drifter.

The OSIC is implied to be worried about creatures of [The Deep](The Deep.md). They seem to know about the [Adversary](The Deep.md) and are aware of how dangerous it is. Once the ritual is over, Haze wants

- name: Viscous to tell her everything about the Adversary so the OSIC can prepare in case any more creatures like it come through The Deep's [Astral Gate](Astral Gates.md).

The OSIC's main office is not local to New York. Instead, they've set up a temporary base of operations in the area, although Haze believes that the OSIC will open a permanent office after the ritual due to all the activity going on in the area.

Haze acknowledges that the OSIC is flawed and that they don't have enough people "who care what doors are getting kicked down". That said, she is still loyal to her job and believes that the ends justify the means. Haze works for the OSIC because she "loves her country".

- Lady Geist is worried she will be sent to an OSIC oubliette if she caters to [Oathkeeper](Oathkeeper.md)'s demands. Haze warns her that the OSIC will have to intervene if "the Oathkeeper issue isn't resolved".

The OSIC is interested in - Kelvin's condition as a revenant. They also show some interest in - name: Celeste being a unicorn.

The OSIC has released a report about hexes.

The OSIC limits expeditions to the North Pole.

## Divisions

The OSIC has multiple divisions. Known divisions include: The Sandman Division The Curator Division The Medium Division 1. Sandman Division A division specialized in infiltrating dreams.

- Haze is a notable member of this division; seeking out the dreams of "persons of interest" to investigate them. If necessary, members of the Sandman Division have clearance to "eliminate" significant detected threats.

The OSIC's Sandmen rarely work together. It's unknown how many Sandmen there are, but Haze says that there's enough of them to call themselves a division.

The Sandman Division has released a holiday calendar to "soften their image", but it only made them seem "more terrifying".

1. Curator Division The Curator Division deals with magical objects like

- name: Pocket's case or - name: Graves ' lich hand. According to - Haze, "Curators care about things and Sandmen care about people".

Haze believes Paige would be a good Curator.

- Paradox wants Haze to put in a good word with the Curator Division for her.
