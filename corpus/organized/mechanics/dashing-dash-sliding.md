- Overview, Strafe Dashing, Dash Jumping, Dash Sliding, Short Dash Crouch Cancelling, Instant Air Dash

- name: Dash Sliding

- file: dashsliding.mp4

- tech_type: Universal Tech

- difficulty: Advanced Easy

- character_dependent: No

- inputs: [Dash](dashing.md) then hold crouch to [slide](sliding.md) [Ground dashes](dashing.md) are eligible to be cancelled into a [slide](sliding.md) at any time. When this is performed, it is called a Dash Slide.

Dash Sliding costs one bar of [stamina](stamina.md).

Dash Sliding is useful for multiple reasons. It: Allows you to maintain some speed out of a regular dash (since a regular dash ends at regular walking speed) and allows you to travel more distance in comparison to a regular dash.

Puts you into the slide state, which is useful for combat and doesn't lock you out of shooting.

Only consumes one bar of stamina, which can be preferable to a [Dash Jump](Dash Jumping.md).

Is easily cancellable, unlike regular dashes which can only be cancelled by a slide.

Dash Sliding for the full duration is not advisable; sliding for the full duration will cause you to eventually move at a speed slower than [walking](walking.md); instead, cancel the slide roughly when the Dash Jump circle turns blue.
