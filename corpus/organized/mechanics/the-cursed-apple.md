The Cursed Apple, also colloquially called The Map or Midtown is the location where all matches takes place in [Deadlock](Deadlock.md).

## Overview

The Cursed Apple is Deadlock's artistic rendition of [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City). The map is divided into a set layout of various themed [Lanes](The Cursed Apple.md#Lanes) and [Districts](The Cursed Apple.md#Districts). The map is asymmetrical, but has similar paths and farms available on both sides.

Players can navigate the map using the [minimap](Minimap.md) in the bottom right corner of the HUD. The minimap will always be flipped so that the player's Patron and base is at the bottom, which may change

- which side lanes and districts are on

- e.g, from left to right, a player on The Hidden King's team would see the map as Yellow - Blue - Green, whereas a player on The Archmother's team would see the map as Green - Blue - Yellow.

.

## Lanes

The map is divided into 3 primary, color-coded lanes: [York](The Cursed Apple.md#York) (Yellow Lane), [Broadway](The Cursed Apple.md#Broadway) (Blue Lane), and [Greenwich](The Cursed Apple.md#Greenwich) (Green Lane). Each lane is further divided into 3 unique locations and contains the game's primary and secondary [objectives](The Cursed Apple.md#Objectives). Each lane has its own [Zipline](Ziplines.md), which shares that lane's respective color.

Ziplines extend from the end of one base to the other, travelling overhead its respective lane and through all of its locations, terminating at the enemy base. How far up a zipline can be traveled on is determined by how far allied Troopers have pushed up, thus creating a constant struggle for map control between the two teams.

## Districts

Outside of the lanes are 4 unique Districts that fill the gaps between: the Theater District, Chinatown District, Haunted Lot District and Plaza District. Lanes and Districts are connected via various buildings, alleyways and tunnels, and are home to various [Haunts](Neutrals.md).

Districts and generally all other alleyways and [tunnels](tunnels.md) connecting the Lanes are also simply referred to as the jungle. This area of the map does not feature any defensive structures (such as Guardians), and does not have any Trooper activity, and is instead full of Haunts, serving as a neutral ground for both teams to wander, farm and fight in.

1. Theater District The Theater District is located on [The Archmother](The Archmother.md)'s side of the map. This district is almost wholly contained within a large theater, filled with a respectable amount of Haunts and breakables. It can be entered at multiple points, including a broken glass roof dome leading into the main auditorium.

1. Chinatown District The Chinatown District is located on [The Hidden King](The Hidden King.md)'s side of the map. This district is decorated with hanging lanterns and East-Asian buildings. Most notably, it contains the Bell Tower, one of two concentrated sources of Souls on the map.

The Bell Tower is a building extending past the height ceiling, and can only be entered or exited via a central [rope](rope.md). At its peak, players can find 3 Sinner's Sacrifices and several Breakables. Attempting to collect any of the Souls in the tower rings its bell, which alerts players across the map to the intruder's presence.

1. Haunted Lot District The Haunted Lot District is located on [The Hidden King](The Hidden King.md)'s side of the map. Aside from a major aesthetic overhaul, this portion of the map is largely the same as it was prior to the [City Never Sleeps](City Never Sleeps.md) update. It now notably features a basketball court with a handful of Haunts on it.

1. Plaza District The Plaza District is located on [The Archmother](The Archmother.md)'s side of the map. This portion of the map features a massive [Fairfax Industries](Fairfax Industries.md) statue, with a stone wall sculpture at its base which emanates a purple fog, which then sinks to the bottom of an underground plaza. Inside the plaza is a sizeable group of Haunts, as well as various breakables, with the purple fog affecting players in 3 major ways: 1. Heroes can not hear their surroundings through the fog layer.

2. Heroes can not see in or out the plaza through the fog layer.

3. [Stamina](Stamina.md) drains slowly over time and does not regenerate.

## Team Bases

1. The Hidden King (Orange) [The Hidden King](The Hidden King.md) is represented by the The Hidden King's Court, colloquially known as the Orange team. His base is a factory filled with large speakers and microphones, and has on overall "run down" and abandoned appearance. The Hidden King appears as a shadowy figure with antlers and glowing yellow eyes, bound to a burning [wicker man](wikipedia_Wicker_man.md) effigy. Long, claw-like yellow hands extend from the shadows.

1. The Archmother (Blue) [The Archmother](The Archmother.md) is represented by the The Guided Hand, colloquially known as the Blue team. Her base is a museum with a [neoclassic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neoclassicism) aesthetic. The Archmother wears a broken mask that resembles the head of the [Statue of Liberty](wikipedia_Statue_of_Liberty.md), her form bound to a crystalline effigy burning with blue flames.

1. Spawns At the rear of each team's base sits the Spawn Room, where heroes [respawn](Death.md) after being killed. It also houses a [Curiosity Shop](The Curiosity Shop.md) and a [Soul Well](Souls.md#Soul-Well), which activates 3 minutes into the match. While inside the room, allied heroes restore Convar

## Objectives

1. Structures (Main Objectives) Along every lane is a series of powerful defensive [structures](structures.md) that must be destroyed in order to advance towards the enemy base. The first line of defense is the [Guardian](Guardian.md), followed by a [Walker](Walker.md) and finally a pair of [Base Guardians](Guardian.md#Base-Guardian). Once inside the enemy base, players must destroy the two [Shrine](Shrine.md)s before they can start damaging the enemy [Patron](Patron.md). Each team has a total of 3 Guardians, 3 Walkers, 6 Base Guardians, 2 Shrines, with the Patron being the last line of defense. A match is won by defeating the enemy Patron, thus completing [The Ritual](The Ritual.md).

1. Midboss In the very middle of the map, in the underground sewer right below Broadway is a major objective known as the [Midboss](Midboss.md). It is a large [Gutter Ghoul](Haunts.md)-like creature which possesses a huge health pool, a constantly regenerating shield, and a high-damage laser beam. After being slain, it will spawn a [Rejuvenator](Rejuvenator.md) Crystal that can be claimed by either team.

1. Unstable Rift The [Unstable Rift](Unstable Rift.md) is a major event that spawns on either Park (Green Lane) or York (Yellow Lane). While the rift usually alternates what side of the map its on, its not uncommon for it to appear on the same side back to back. The objective of the event is King of the Hill: players must remain within the designated area in order to capture the point for a huge soul reward and to summon several [Rift Troopers](Trooper.md#Rift_Troopers) to aid them in their attack on the enemy objectives.

Both teams can simultaneously gain capture progress, but will not be able to claim the rewards while contested. A team's progress will not exceed 99% as long as at least 1 enemy player is present within the area. They must first be killed, or otherwise displaced with an ability or scared off as so they are no longer within the capture area. Troopers can not contest the objective.

Should the rift go unclaimed or be contested for too long, it will fade away and will instead release [Haunted Souls](Souls.md#Haunted-Souls). An expired rift does not spawn Rift Troopers.

1. Soul Urn The [Soul Urn](Soul Urn.md) is a minor objective that always first spawns on York (Yellow Lane). After that, it alternates: Park (Green Lane), then York (Yellow Lane), and so on. It can be picked up by any player and must be delivered to the opposite side (if it spawned on Yellow, it must be brought to Green, and vice versa) within a specified time frame.

The urn carrier receives temporary buffs to make the journey easier, but may not use Ziplines, [Teleporter](Teleporter.md)s, Tunnel [Vent](Vent.md)s, and some of their abilities (depending on the hero). Successfully delivering the urn to its destination will award the carrier with a large amount of haunted souls, which may be denied. The carrier is also awarded 4 random permanent stat buffs.

The carrier can be forced to drop the urn by taking [melee](melee.md) damage or being [stun](stun.md)ned. If the Soul Urn is not dropped off in time, it will disappear without granting any rewards. Should nobody pick up the Soul Urn after several minutes, it will start walking towards its destination by itself, but may still be picked up along the way. Likewise, it may be abandoned elsewhere on the map.

The Soul Urn continuously taunts nearby players to pick it up and deliver it. While carried, it makes various comments about the hero carrying it, as well as telling them to hurry up.

## Interactibles

Various props are scattered throughout the map that can be interacted with by the players. While the majority can be found in the various districts and jungle, some also appear directly in lanes. The term "going to farm" is used to indicate you're about to leave your lane to head off into the jungle to loot crates, buff containers, Sinner's Sacrifices and kill Haunts.

1. Breakables Throughout the map, players can find breakables in the form of [Crate](Crate.md)s and [Buff Containers](Buff Container.md). Crates have a chance to drop [Souls](Souls.md) upon being destroyed, while Buff Containers a chance to drop permanent stat bonuses, such as [Spirit Power](Spirit Power.md). The majority of breakables first spawn in at 3 minutes into the game although, some exceptions apply (see [Map Timings & Events](The Cursed Apple.md#Map-Timings-&-Events)) 1. Sinner's Sacrifice [Sinner's Sacrifice](Sinner's Sacrifice.md) machines appear in various locations on the map. Unlike crates and buff containers, they award both souls and permanent stat buffs when destroyed. They are generally considered high-value interactibles, but require significantly more time to collect and may attract unwanted attention. They first appear 8:00 minutes into the game.

1. Bridge Buffs The map features two "bridges" in which temporary [buff](buff.md)s spawn on every 5:00 minute interval. They may be claimed by either team.

1. Healing Snacks Players may find [Healing Snack](Healing Snack.md)s at various locations throughout the map, which take the appearance of a green, glass apple with wings. Usually found in high or hard to get places, these can be picked up by observant players for a for a small heal during a fight or while retreating.

## Map Features

The map is also filled with various other points of interest and interactive elements.

1. Curiosity Shop The map contains 10 [Curiosity Shop](Curiosity Shop.md)s, one alongside each [Guardian](Guardian.md), which may only be used by the team on whose side it is, two "secret" shops which are neutral and may be used by either team, and one inside each team's spawn room. Players can visit these shops to spend [Souls](Souls.md) by buying [Items](Items.md).

1. Teleporters The map features 4 [Teleporter](Teleporter.md)s, which are linked in pairs. Players can use them to quickly travel to the opposite side of the map. Players should note that teleporting isn't instantaneous, and that there is a short wind-up time, allowing potential enemies in pursuit to catch up and possibly kill them.

1. Ropes Ropes can be found in various buildings, or on their outsides. They allow vertical travel onto rooftops and different floors of buildings.

1. Jump Pads Jump pads take the appearance of [Air Vent](Air Vent.md)s. When stepped on, they will launch the hero in the direction of the airflow. They're predominatly used as a sort of speed boost, enabling mostly horizontal travel, rather than launching players upwards to reach high places.

1. Tunnel Vents Certain heroes, such as Rat King, Rem, or heroes using certain abilities like Calico, can make use of various vents to enter the [tunnel](tunnel.md)s beneath the map, allowing for faster travel between two points.

1. Steam Vents [Steam Vents](Steam Vents.md) can be found scattered throughout the map. Players can stand on them to become invisible to enemy players, also becoming untargetable by abilities and items (that explicitly require a target to cast), and receive a small health regeneration boost.

1. Juke Rooms Juke Rooms are small dead-end rooms located in various locations around the map. They are marked prominently by a shadowy figure standing in their doorways that contain a [Cosmic Veil](Cosmic Veil.md). Above the doorways is a red rectangular sign, and the compartment contains candles and a small wall light.

Juke Rooms - as implied by their name - are most often used to hide or ambush enemies, as they have only one entrance which you can only see through from the inside. The doorway shadow also acts as a warning for fleeing players that they are about to walk into a dead-end.

## Territory

An important part of [Deadlock](Deadlock.md) is managing the clash of [Trooper](Trooper.md)s that occurs in each lane. Killing enemy Troopers allows allied Troopers to move further into the lane. This extends the [Zipline](Ziplines.md) available to the player's team on that lane. The Ziplines on the map will show how far a lane is "pushed" towards the enemy base, how far the Zipline can take the player, and if any lanes are in need of help with a quick glance.

[Teleporter](Teleporter.md)s can be used to quickly traverse the map horizontally.
