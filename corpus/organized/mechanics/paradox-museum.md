The Paradox Museum is a pop-up gallery showcasing everything the Paradox Organisation steals.

## Summary

[The Paradox Organisation](Paradox Organisation.md) steals priceless artifacts to be showcased at the Paradox Museum, with the intention of showing that anything can be stolen by Paradox. Once a sufficient number of artifacts are gathered, the museum is open to the public for a short amount of time.

The Paradox Museum isn't exclusively for art: it's also used to showcase stolen state secrets, celebrity paternity tests, and forms of blackmail on renowned individuals.

As a pop-up gallery, the Paradox Museum has no set location. Where Paradox stores their artifacts while the Museum is closed is unknown.
