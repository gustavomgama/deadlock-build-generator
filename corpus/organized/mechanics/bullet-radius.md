Bullet Radius or Bullet Size is a [stat](stat.md) that determines the size of a player's bullets, in centimeters or inches. It cannot be increased with items (with the exception of the [Street Brawl](Street Brawl.md) item Haunting Shot, which makes the player fire an oversized bullet).
