## Summary

Brought about by an eclipse, the first Maelstrom presumably occurred circa the [1880s-1890s](Storyline.md). This Maelstrom brought a large influx of spirits back to the mortal world, one of which included Vindicta, and caused some dormant [Astral Gates](Astral Gates.md) to open, including one in New York's Central Park. This was the event that introduced the general public to the supernatural.

50 years later, in the present day, a second Maelstrom is about to occur.

The duration of the Maelstrom is expected to last between 20 minutes to 2 hours, during which mystic energy is at its apex.

The third Maelstrom isn't expected to happen for another 50 years, but it could happen earlier.

## Ritual

The ritual is an event that takes place during the second Maelstrom, in which two [teams](teams.md) of six (or more) individuals are fighting to summon a [Patron](Patron.md), an [extraplanar](Outer Planes.md) elder god, who can grant them a wish. The ritual can only be completed once the enemy team's Patron is destroyed.

- Haze has prepared extensively for the ritual by performing drills, research, stretching, divination and fortune telling.

- name: Warden has trained his whole life for the ritual.

Death isn't permanent during the ritual.
