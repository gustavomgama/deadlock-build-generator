- pic: Melee damage

- size: 32 is a stat that [Heroes](Hero.md) start with and determines how much damage a hero's [Melee Attack](Melee Attack.md) deals.

Melee damage [scales](Scaling.md) with [Boon](Boon.md)s and [Item](Item.md)s. It also scales with the [Weapon Damage](Weapon Damage.md) stat at a rate of 50%. Most abilities and items with weapon damage effects also affect melee damage, but there are exceptions such as - Metal Skin.

Most heroes have the same base melee damage and scaling, with a few exceptions (see table below).
