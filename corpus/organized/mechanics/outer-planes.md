The Outer Planes refer to planes of existence beyond the known universe.

Some Outer Planes are connected to Earth by [Astral Gates](Astral Gates.md).

## Summary

The Outer Planes include [Ixia](Ixia.md) and the [Patrons'](Patron.md) plane. Some souls of humans move to an Outer Plane after death.

The US is currently sending aid to the Outer Planes due to the military campaign in Ixia.

- The Doorman is an extraplanar being and an "old friend" of the Patrons.

An exhibit at the Met is due to open up showing off artifacts from the Outer Planes.

The [New York](The Cursed Apple.md) [Astral Gate](Astral Gate.md) has led to extraplanar invasive species appearing in the city.

[Astral Plane Canned Mille-Pattes Dazzle](Brands.md) ("mille-pattes" is French for "centipede") are advertised as The World's Most Popular Delicacy.
