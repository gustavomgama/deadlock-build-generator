List of known [movement tech](Character Tech.md) for Ivy.

## Stone Form

is - Ivy's third ability. In it, she turns into an invulnerable rock, briefly jumping into the air before slamming into the ground. Mechanically, Stone Form retains some of Ivy's horizontal movement, jumps to its calculated height and ends 0.5 seconds after she hits the ground. By using these mechanical quirks, Ivy can perform movement tech to optimize her time spent in Stone Form. Stone Form is one of the few abilities that can execute a [Heavy Melee Cancel](Heavy Melee_Heavy Melee Cancel.md).

1. Stalling Stone Form does not end until Ivy hits the ground. Certain spots on the map can be stood on without being considered the ground (ex. chimneys, certain vehicles, and so on). By landing Stone Form in these spots, Ivy can delay leaving Stone Form. granting her extra time in invulnerability to call for help or wait out her cooldowns. This can be used to circumvent the damage cooldown on - Air Drop.

1. Super Jump Stone Form calculates its maximum height based on the height Ivy was at when it was cast. This calculated height is a world coordinate, and not a set distance. By traveling through a - Doorway or otherwise teleporting mid-cast, Ivy can perform an abnormally high jump as the game tries to reach the originally calculated height.

## Air Drop

- name: Air Drop

- file: Ivyairdrop.mp4

- tech_type: Character Tech

- difficulty: Universal Basics

- character_dependent: Yes

- name: IAD Trajectory

- tech_type: Character Tech

- file: ivyiadtrajectory.mp4

- inputs: immediately as you enter launch phase 1. IAD Trajectory This trajectory puts you as close as you can get to the ground. This is executed by instantly dashing as you enter launch phase. This can be useful in indoors environments.

- name: Backwards Loop Trajectory

- tech_type: Character Tech

- file: ivybackwardstrajectory.mp4

- inputs: Buffer movement key while in launch phase 1. Backwards Loop This trajectory allows you to initially travel backwards when emerging from the launch phase into the fly phase. This can be executed by buffering the backwards movement key while launching. You will travel up vertically, then fly backwards for a second and loop around back forwards. For the most part, this is a novelty. Uses include messing with opponents and trolling.
