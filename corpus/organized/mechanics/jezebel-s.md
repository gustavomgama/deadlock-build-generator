Jezebel's Billiard Hall is a sketchy yet popular bar located in New York.

## Summary

Jezebel's is an old church renovated into a billiard's hall and bar. The place is run by a man named [Hank](Minor characters.md) and tended to by - Infernus, who doubles as the bar's bouncer.

Jezebel's is described in The New York Oracle as a place where people can bet their souls over a game of pool.

Shortly before the events of the game take place, Jezebel's is under threat of getting shut down by the Alchemy Bureau due to a large number of infractions, including gambling souls without a license, distributing spirits from Ixia without a permit, and not having the Hex & Alchemy Bureau authorize a ward at the bar's entrance. Infernus and Hank are given 2 weeks to resolve the infractions.

1. Hank Hank is a portly, middle-aged man who owns Jezebel's. He's worried about the bar's infractions and is willing to "make a deal with the devil himself" in order to save his bar.

The threat of Jezebel's being shut down and what it means for Hank is why Infernus is participating in the ritual. Since Infernus doesn't want Hank to worry about him, he hasn't told Hank about his involvement with the ritual.

Infernus mentions that Hank "saved his life". The implication seems to be that Hank helped Infernus move on from his criminal youth and turn over a new leaf as a bartender.

Hank is also implied to be friends with - name: Celeste, Lady Geist, and - name: Mo & Krill.

In one pathway in [The Binding of Oathkeeper](The Binding of Oathkeeper.md), Lady Geist pawns off Oathkeeper to Hank on the promise of getting Hank the resources needed to save Jezebel's.
