- name: Base Turret

- max_health: ∞

- dps: 288 Base Turrets, also known as Fountain Turrets, are an indestructible defense [unit](unit.md) in [Deadlock](Deadlock.md).

## Overview

There are 4 Base Turrets per team: two facing each room to the side of the team's spawn area and two facing inwards inside the spawn area.

Base Turrets will target enemies close to the spawn and can see up to 90° to their sides. Their damage increases the closer the enemy gets, up to 288 [Weapon Damage](Weapon Damage.md) DPS (144 per shot) inside the spawn, mitigated by [Bullet Resist](Bullet Resist.md).

Base Turrets will not target an enemy while they're being pushed by - The Doorman's Luggage Cart.

Base Turrets' [fire rate](fire rate.md) is affected by - Heroic Aura.

Base Turrets' bullets are affected by items such as - Metal Skin, Plated Armor, and - Return Fire. Return Fire can hit enemy heroes, but Plated Armor destroys incoming bullets and does not reflect them.
