This page has a glossary of gameplay terms commonly used by the Deadlock community. Most of these terms have been adopted from other MOBA games.

## A-Z

AOE Initialism for "area of effect".

Assassin A type of hero who specializes in ambushing enemy heroes, typically being particularly good at 1v1s.

Backdoor Attacking an objective without the attacker's troopers reaching it first.

Baron The equivalent term to - Mid-Boss in League of Legends.

BKB Initialism for [Black King Bar](https://liquipedia.net/dota2/Black_King_Bar), Dota's equivalent of - Unstoppable.

BM Initialism for "bad manners".

BRB Initialism for "be right back".

Burst Dealing a massive amount of damage in a very short window of time.

Carry A type of hero who scales well with [Souls](Souls.md) and therefore is able to overpower most threats in the late game.

CC Initialism for "Crowd Control", referring to [Status Effects](Status Effects.md) that limit a hero's ability to act or move (e.g., Stuns, Silences, Slows, and Roots).

CD [Cooldown](Cooldown.md) Creep The equivalent term to - Trooper in Dota. Rarely used to refer to - Denizen.

CS Initialism for - Counterspell. May also refer for "Creep Score." Borrowed from Dota and League of Legends, heavily used to refer to the number of - Trooper

- l1: Troopers and - Denizen

- l1: Denizens a player has killed.

Debuff Remover Old name for - Dispel Magic.

Deny The act of shooting the soul orb of a dying enemy Trooper before the enemy can, stealing the souls for yourself.

DoT [Status Effects](Status Effects.md) that deal constant damage over time.

Doubles Refers to [camps](Haunts.md) which contain two [Sinner's Sacrifice](Sinner's Sacrifice.md) machines.

DPS Initialism for "damage per second".

Feed Intentionally or unintentionally giving the enemy team souls.

Fountain Spawn room.

Frontliner A type of hero who is relatively durable and seeks to disrupt enemy players.

Gank Ambushing one or more enemies, usually by rotating out of your assigned lane to create a numbers advantage.

Hook Bebop's Grapple Arm. Rarely used to refer to - Yamato's Flying Slash.

Int Short for "intentional feeding", or when a player griefs by intentionally dying to the other team.

Jungle The area of the map between lanes, or the act of farming Denizen

- l1: Denizens (neutral camps) within those areas.

M1 Short for "Mouse 1", commonly referring to [Weapon](Weapon.md)-based heroes/builds.

Mid Mid-Boss. Sometimes used to refer to blue lane.

Minion The equivalent term to - Trooper in League of Legends and Smite.

Missing A call-out used to warn teammates that an enemy Hero is unaccounted for, and may be ready to gank or ambush unsuspecting players.

Patron The final core objective inside the enemy base. Equivalent to the "Ancient" in Dota or "Nexus" in League of Legends.

Pick Killing an isolated Hero, usually to keep them from helping with an upcoming team fight.

Power Spike A point in the game where a hero becomes significantly stronger, due to unlocking a specific ability upgrade, item, or bonus points in their build.

Proc Short for "procedure" or "programmed random occurrence", meaning an effect that has a random chance to be triggered under specific circunstances.

Push/Pushing Advancing a lane by killing enemy Trooper

- l1: Troopers and objectives.

Rat/Ratting When a player ignores ongoing team fights in favor of pushing waves/objectives. Also known as "split-pushing".

Remlings Rem's Lil Helpers.

Skillshot An ability that requires aiming to hit.

Snowball Using an early-game advantage to gain exponential momentum, becoming highly over-leveled and dominating the rest of the match.

Squishy A hero with low maximum health and [physical/spirit resistance](Damage Resistance.md), making them very easy to kill quickly.

Support A type of hero who primarily aids their teammates with heals, buffs, or CC.

T1 / T2 Shorthand for objective tiers. T1 refers to - Guardian

- l1: Guardians and T2 refers to - Walker

- l1: Walkers.

Tank A type of hero who can absorb large amounts of damage at once.

Throw Giving advantage to the initially losing team.

Tower The equivalent term to - Guardian in Dota.

TP Initialism for "Teleport", commonly used to refer to the [Teleporters](Teleporter.md) scattered across the map and - Mirage's Traveler.

Turret McGinnis ' Mini Turret. Also the equivalent term to - Guardian in League of Legends.

Ult Short for "Ultimate", referring to a hero's fourth ability.

Urn / Spirit Urn The - Soul Urn

- l1: objective that periodically spawns and must be carried to a drop-off point on the opposite side of the map to grant souls to the carrier.

Wave Refers to a deployment of - Trooper

- l1: Troopers on a lane.

Zip [Ziplines](Ziplines.md)
