## Overview

The digital book [The Final Hours of Half-Life Alyx](https://combineoverwiki.net/wiki/The_Final_Hours_of_Half-Life:_Alyx) (2020) mentioned "a top-secret new project that another small team at Valve has been working on since the first part of 2018".

References to Citadel in other Valve games were first discovered by dataminers in 2019. It was speculated to be a multiplayer team-based shooter set in the [Half-Life](wikipedia_Half-Life_(video_game).md) universe, named after the [Combine Citadel](https://combineoverwiki.net/wiki/Citadel) from Half-Life 2. It was also speculated to feature asymmetrical VR-desktop gameplay.

[Yoshi](Yoshi.md) shared gameplay footage of an early Citadel prototype build that used [Half-Life: Alyx](wikipedia_Half-Life__Alyx.md) and [Left 4 Dead 2](wikipedia_Left_4_Dead_2.md) assets.

The two teams in Citadel initially were named Team Nick and Team Coach, after the characters from Left 4 Dead 2.
