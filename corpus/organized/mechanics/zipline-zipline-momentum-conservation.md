- inputs: Be on the [Transit Line](Ziplines.md) then press the - Zipline Momentum Conservation Also known as ZMC is a technique that relies on the [transit lines](Ziplines.md) running throughout the three lanes. By dismounting the Transit Lines using crouch, you can travel further.

ZMC takes no bars of [stamina](stamina.md).

When dismounting from the Transit Lines, you are granted a buff that disables regular air drag, after which you have 5% extra air dragNormal air drag has a friction coefficient of 0.2; dropping from the transit line gives a friction coefficient of 0, then 0.25 after two seconds. This can be used to conserve speed from the Transit Line.

Some characters can capitalize on this using abilities that have cast times (abilities with a timer at the bottom), maintaining momentum and allowing for higher offensive and defensive options. These characters include: Dynamo Ivy Lash Pocket Grey Talon Vindicta Warden Yamato Any character in the cast can perform ZMC to gain the air drag buff.

To use ZMC, start holding the jump button to latch onto the transit line. During any point in the ascent from your starting position to the line, press the duck key. The buff will be applied, and you will be fully actionable in the air.
