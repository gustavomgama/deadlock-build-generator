## Alt-fire

Shiv does not have a zoom. Instead, he has an alt-fire (RMB or M2).

Shiv’s alt-fire shoots

- get_alt_fire_var Shiv AmmoConsumedPerShot rounds out of his shotgun. The recoil knocks you back, similar to the weapon [Force-A-Nature](https://wiki.teamfortress.com/wiki/Force-a-Nature) from Team Fortress 2. This alt-fire allows for more movement opportunities for Shiv.

Alt-fire sends you at an initial impulse of 400 units per second, after which you are subject to gravity and air drag. The amount of horizontal and vertical velocity you get out of alt-fire are components of the inital impulse and the angle you shot at.

If you're on the ground and you do not aim parallel to the ground, your horizontal velocity will be the component of the impulse and the aiming angle, but your vertical impulse will be eaten, resulting in less horizontal velocity.

When on the ground, usage of alt-fire will result in a vertical impulse of 12 units upwards. This results in Shiv being able to slide easily from an alt-fire by utilising Slide Storage.

Alt-fire always sends at 404 units per second; depending on your pre-existing velocity, alt-fire can be detrimental.

- name: M2 Recoil Skip

- file: Shivrecoilskip.mp4

- tech_type: Character Tech

- difficulty: Universal Basic

- inputs: Alt-fire → Ground dash 1. M2 Recoil Skip By inputting alt-fire followed immediately by a [ground dash](Dashing.md) to move in the direction of your dash, you can ignore the backwards momentum from alt-fire. This is called a **M2 Recoil Skip**. This can be useful when chasing enemies.

Note that this only works with ground dashes and not air dashes. Ground dashes directly set your velocity, while air dashes add to your existing velocity.

- name: M2 Wall Jump

- file: Shivm2walljump.mp4

- tech_type: Character Tech

- difficulty: Adanced Easy

- inputs: Alt-fire → Wall Jump 1. M2 Wall Jump M2 Wall Jump is the act of inputting alt-fire and then immediately wall jumping to gain the velocity boost from both the recoil from Shiv’s alt-fire in addition to the [wall jump](Wall Jumping.md). To execute, aim towards a wall, alt-fire, and then press jump immediately after, ideally after one tick. Like with any wall jump, you can hold a direction to influence your wall jump trajectory while performing this technique.

This technique can be used to gain significant velocity away from the wall.

The input can be inconsistent depending on the geometry of the wall. If you don’t input the wall jump quickly enough after alt-fire, you’ll be too far away from the wall to wall jump and will perform an air jump instead, but if you press alt-fire and wall jump at the same time, alt-fire will sometimes not register due to the wall jump locking out inputs.

Firing alt-fire at a shallower angle to the wall can make this easier to perform at the cost of gaining less speed; this lets you move more laterally along the wall. It is also easier to do this on some walls than others - the flatter and more plain the wall, the easier.

Anecdotally, binding alt-fire and jump to keys on the same hand (e.g. both on keyboard or both on mouse buttons) can make the execution easier.

- name: M2 Sliding

- file: Shivm2slide.mp4

- tech_type: Character Tech

- difficulty: Advanced Easy

- inputs: Alt-fire→ Slide (on ground, don’t press slide immediately after the M2; wait ~0.1 seconds) 1. M2 Sliding M2 Sliding is the act of using Shiv’s alt-fire to enter into the [slide](Sliding.md) state.

Shiv’s alt-fire knocks him back at a peak speed of 404 u/s in the ground and in the air. You can use this speed to start a slide, since the speed exceeds the 350 u/s threshold needed to start a slide.

Without items, you are only able to enter slide state for one shot; see Infinite M2 Slide to be able to continuously enter slide state while shooting.

To execute M2 Sliding, use alt-fire and slide. When shooting, try and shoot parallel to the ground to get the most speed and acceleration out of the alt-fire shot.

- name: M2 Slide Hopping

- file: Shivm2slidehop.mp4

- tech_type: Character Tech

- difficulty: Advanced Hard

- inputs: (Move at speed) → Slide → Alt-fire → Jump → Repeat 1. M2 Slide Hopping M2 Slide Hopping is a technique where you are able to [slide Bunnyhop](Bunnyhopping_Slide Bunnyhopping.md) with alt-fire. This can be done without any items, but is harder than Infinite M2 Sliding. The main advantage of M2 Slide Hopping is that it does not take any items at all and is faster than regular base walking speed. For these reasons, M2 Slide Hopping is good during the laning phase.

M2 Slide Hopping can be used for faster general traversal around the map or to make a slightly faster getaway if you are out of [stamina](stamina.md).

The tradeoff is that M2 Slide Hopping without items is hard to do without consuming ammo; the timing is tricky, but can be mastered.

To M2 Slide Hop (assuming no items): First, move with enough speed to start a [slide](Sliding.md) and be in the air. The easiest way to gain this speed is to either [Instant Air Dash](Dashing_Instant Air Dash.md) or [Dash Jump](Dashing_Dash Jumping.md); both are valid.

While in the air, Face roughly perpendicular to where you want to go. (e.g. face left or right if you want to go forward) Hold the movement key that results in forward movement in your current camera position (e.g. hold D if you are facing left of where you want to go.) Land and slide. The easiest way to slide is to hold the duck key and buffer slide during steps 2a-3, to activate [Slide Conservation](Sliding.md).

Nearly immediately after sliding, use alt-fire to gain momentum. Slide for ~.5 seconds Switch movement keys smoothly to the forward movement key. (e.g. If you were holding the left movement key, shift over to the forward movement key.) While doing this, move your camera to the forward direction.

[Jump](Jumping.md) and repeat steps 2a-4.

- name: Infinite M2 Slide

- file: Shivinfm2slide.mp4

- tech_type: Character Tech

- difficulty: Advanced Hard

- inputs: Slide → alt-fire (can be buffered by holding right click) 1. Infinite M2 Slide Infinite M2 Slide requires items to be bought from the shop that increase fire rate. A minimum of +22% fire rate is required, but a higher percentage is recommended, and will result in higher leniency, speed and control. It can also be done with the item Fleetfoot which increases [slide](Sliding.md) distance by 35% on its own.

Using this faster fire rate, you can chain M2 shots while in a [slide](Sliding.md) to maintain or even gain speed that exceeds the threshold of 350 u/s.

Furthermore, slides allow for infinite shooting, allowing you to slide infinitely with no ammo loss, and if done from a ground [jump](Jumping.md), costs no bars of [stamina](stamina.md). This allows the slide to go on forever as long as you keep shooting. The Infinite M2 Slide also allows you to make sharp turns with the slide.

The Infinite M2 Slide itself does not cost any stamina.

You cannot initiate an Infinite M2 Slide from a [dash slide](Dashing_Dash Sliding.md); when shooting, you will exit out of the slide.

You can initiate an Infinite M2 Slide out of an M2 Slide; however, the inputs are very tight (to execute an Infinite M2 Slide out of an M2 Slide, press crouch and alt-fire at the same time).

You can slide in one of two ways: Backwards: This has limited combat potential due to facing backwards to where you want to go. Sliding backwards requires holding the backwards movement key (by default, S).

Sideways: This has the most combat potential due to your crosshair being closer to the forward direction. This is the only way that you can start accelerating using the Infinite M2 Slide. Sliding sideways requires holding either the left or right movement key (by default, A or D).

To execute Infinite M2 Sliding, be in a slide, hold a directional movement key and hold M2 to it to be buffered and automatically time it for you. The easiest way to initiate an Infinite M2 Slide is to jump and hold the duck key, then shoot.

- name: Infinite M2 Slide Acceleration

- file: Shivinfm2slideaccel.mp4

- tech_type: Character Tech

- difficulty: Advanced Hard

- stamina: 0-1

- inputs: Slide (Continue to hold slide) → alt-fire (can be buffered by holding right click) 1. Infinite M2 Slide Acceleration Infinite M2 Slide Acceleration is a technique where you Infinite M2 Slide sideways and shoot perpendicular to where you are going.

For there to be a noticeable amount of acceleration you need either A minimum fire rate of around 40%, A minimum fire rate of 12% with - Fleetfoot or - Burst Fire.

With Infinite M2 Slide Acceleration, you can reach peak speeds of ~1300 u/s, although the highest speed you will practically reach is ~900 u/s.

Infinite M2 Slide Acceleration is useful for traversing the map and quickly escaping fights downhill. As with the Infinite M2 Slide, this can either cost no stamina and two rounds, one bar of stamina, or nothing if done on a ramp.

To execute, first start an Infinite M2 Slide sideways, then shoot perpendicular to where you are moving.

## Slice and Dice

1. Slice and Dice Leap Slice and Dice is Shiv's second ability, in which he dashes 12.37 metres or 487 units forward, dealing Spirit damage to opponents in the way. Slice and Dice ignores pre-existing momentum and velocity and ends you with zero momentum; Slice and Dice can be thought of as a set velocity sequence.

Slice and Dice can be aimed in nearly every direction, with exception of directly overhead and beneath, which will instead send at a 89° angle. This can be used for traversal as a "second" dash.

Slice and Dice disregards all prior momentum and stops you with zero velocity; Slice and Dice is best used at the very end of a chain of actions or at a rollout. Slice and Dice is also disabled by - Slowing Hex, potentially making rollouts utilising Slice and Dice unfeasible when chased.
