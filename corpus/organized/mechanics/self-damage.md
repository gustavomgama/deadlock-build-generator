Self-damage is damage dealt by a [Hero](Heroes.md) to themself, through [abilities](abilities.md), [items](items.md), or their own weapon.

## Properties

Self-damage bypasses all [barriers](Damage Barrier.md) (except for Rat King's Royal Pestments ) and is typically non-lethal. Like other non-lethal damage, it will still trigger Victor's Shocking Reanimation and Cheat Death.

Currently, all sources of self-damage are either [spirit damage](Spirit Damage.md) or [pure damage](Pure Damage.md). Spirit self-damage can be reduced by [Spirit Resist](Spirit Resist.md). Incoming [damage amplification](Damage Amplification.md) affects only spirit self-damage. However, incoming [damage reduction](Damage Reduction.md) affects both kinds of self-damage. Outgoing damage amp or reduction does not affect self-damage at all.
