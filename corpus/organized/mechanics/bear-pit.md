The Bear Pit is an underground arena of blood sport in New York where spectators can bet on prize fights.

## Summary

Known fighters include Lash and - Bebop, who are rivals. Lash calls himself a "champion" of the Bear Pit.

Lash has lost to Bebop in the Bear Pit once, while Bebop lost to Lash at least once afterwards.

- Wraith and - name: Mo & Krill

- alt_name: Krill are prominent gamblers in matches, who are both known to often bet money on Bebop. Once, [Mendoza](The Mendoza Syndicate.md) told Wraith that Lash was going to "take a dive" in a Bear Pit fight, which Lash proceeded to win, causing Wraith to lose "a lot of money".

Bebop is fighting in the Bear Pit to earn money for [Ms. Shelly](Shelly Fisher.md)'s medicine. He intends to retire once he completes the [Ritual](The Ritual.md) and gets his wish.

- name: Celeste's friend [Blockhead Lenny](Minor characters.md#Blockhead-Lenny) once fought in the Bear Pit, but lost to Lash.

Fights in the Bear Pit seem to allow the use of magic.
