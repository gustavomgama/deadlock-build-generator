Boons in [Deadlock](Deadlock.md) indicate the player's power level over the course of the match. Players gain boons by gathering [Souls](Souls.md), which unlocks [Ability Points](Ability Points.md) and increases their [Weapon Damage](Weapon Damage.md), [Melee Damage](Melee Damage.md), [Health](Health.md) and [Spirit Power](Spirit Power.md). The soul count is always visible to all players under their portrait, and the boon level can be seen in the soul container icon next to the player's soul count, and over the player's portrait by pressing Tab. The soul container fills up as the player collects souls, indicating how close they are to the next boon.

## Ability Points

[Ability points](Ability Point.md) are obtained as level up rewards. Each ability has 3 tiers, requiring 1, 2, and then 5 ability points to unlock. It is possible to spend ability points in any order, including saving them to spend on an ultimate ability or for the final level of an ability.

See [Ability Points and Boon Thresholds](ability-points.md) for the concrete Boon/souls table.

## Boon Rewards

Boon Rewards serve to increase the base attributes of the hero. Level ups always grant a Boon Reward.

The base attributes that are improved by Boons are: base [bullet damage](Bullet Damage.md), base [melee damage](Melee Damage.md), [spirit power](Spirit Power.md), and base [health](health.md). Each hero gains unique amounts of base stats from their Boons. The differences in these boon profiles can dictate which heroes are more powerful in the early stages of a game and which heroes become powerful in the late game. Some heroes may have additional stats improved by Boons, such as - Dynamo's [bullet resist](bullet resist.md).

## Boon Scaling

Some [Abilities](Abilities.md) and [Items](Items.md) have boon scaling properties, indicated by a green arrow and the hero's icon. Boon scaling properties increase based on the player's boon level.
