## Summary

Described as "controversial", "a neo inquisition" and "an extremist neopolitical movement", the Friends of Humanity have an infamous reputation for being bigoted towards the supernatural.

- Vindicta wants to exact revenge on John Hathorne's descendants and eradicate the Friends of Humanity.

- Grey Talon suspects the group was behind the murder of his family, and is similarly pursuing them. Both Grey Talon and Vindicta are working together in their quest for revenge.

The Friends of Humanity were founded by the descendants of [John Hathorne](wikipedia_John_Hathorne.md), a judge in the real [Salem Witch Trials](wikipedia_Salem_witch_trials.md) who was known for questioning accusers and the accused in trial, often leaving the accused guilty. Currently, the only named descendant is John Hathorne Jr., who acts as the leader of the Friends of Humanity. John Hathorne Jr. also had an unnamed brother who was assassinated by Vindicta. News has gone around about known associates of John Hathorne Jr. and the Friends of Humanity ending up dead.

The Friends of Humanity have made public speeches and rallies in the past. They have recently announced a rally to protest against the [United States](United States of America.md) sending aid to the [Outer Planes](Outer Planes.md).

- Apollo mentions a rally in Boston where Grey Talon seemed to have murdered the security for the movement. Similarly, Vindicta assassinated someone running the Boston Marathon.

Vindicta isn't worried about running out of time to wipe out the Friends of Humanity due to her undead state, and states that John Hathorne Jr. and "his little merry band of bigots" will die at her leisure.

- Haze warns Vindicta that murdering John Hathorne would galvanize the Friends of Humanity, advising her to quit while she was ahead.

- Mo & Krill don't like the Friends of Humanity and appreciate Grey Talon's actions against them. Similarly, Infernus isn't fussed about Vindicta's war against them. Apollo commends Vindicta for her war against the Friends of Humanity. None of the others heroes seem to like the Friends of Humanity, although some characters take issue with Vindicta and Grey Talon's murderous methods.

Vindicta's exploits have become known in [Ixia](Ixia.md). According to Apollo, Vindicta is called "Hasha Vogali", which is Ixian for "Death's Whisper".

1. John Hathorne Jr.

The current leader of the Friends of Humanity, John Hathorne Jr. is well known as a "political firebrand" and a "controversial speaker".

John Hathorne Jr. seems the primary target of Vindicta and Grey Talon, having already survived an assassination attempt on his life. Vindicta mentions that Hathorne is in hiding. Despite this, Hathorne intends to make a public appearance at Columbia College shortly after the ritual, where Vindicta intends to assassinate him should she fail the ritual.

John Hathorne Jr. has had an exclusive interview with the [New York Oracle](The New York Oracle.md).
