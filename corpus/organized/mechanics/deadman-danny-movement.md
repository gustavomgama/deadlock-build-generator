## Rocket Launcher (LMB)

By holding down the left mouse button for more than 52 ticks or 0.8 seconds you can charge up to release a rocket. On contact with a wall or enemy, this rocket will explode with a knockback radius of 5 metres for you, and 2.75 metres for opponents (allies cannot be affected by this knockback) The rocket travels at 700 u/s. The rocket does variable damage depending on how far the opponent is from the centre of the explosion. A direct hit gives the maximum amount of damage.

The impulse is dependant on a few factors: If you are crouching, the knockback multiplier to yourself is 1.12. This applies even if you are airborne; you just have to hold crouch.

If the rocket hits a wall and you are airborne, the knockback multiplier is 0.5.

The base impulse is a range between 550 (when furthest away from the centre) and 610 (when closest to the centre) units per second not accounting for air drag or gravity.

If you shoot directly down on the ground below you, the max velocity is 593 u/s with ±2 units variance.

If you shoot directly down on the ground below you whilst crouching, the max velocity is 668 u/s ±2 units variance. (not 682 due to gravity and air drag) If you shoot parallel to the ground (i.e. straight forward) towards a wall, the max velocity is 600 u/s. This also pops you into the air.

If you shoot parallel to the ground towards a wall whilst crouching, the max velocity is 670 u/s. This also pops you into the air.

If you shoot parallel to the ground towards a wall whilst in the air, the max velocity is 300 u/s.

If you shoot parallel to the ground towards a wall whilst in the air and crouching, the max velocity is 330 u/s.

Aiming anywhere above parallel with the ground will result in no “jump”. Aiming anywhere below 90° will result in more horizontal impulse being converted to vertical impulse.

Rockets originate from the centre of the character (their (0,0,0)), meaning that when you fire a rocket, it will not come from the centre of your screen, but instead slightly below, since the camera is raised from their centre. Unlike TF2, the rocket does not have left side bias.

When you are disarmed you are unable to use the rocket. You can still shoot rockets while silenced.

The velocity of a rocket can stack with other sources of velocity (e.g., walljumps, dashes); with this, rockets become extremely useful as a movement tool to cross distance and very quickly escape situations.

Rockets are fairly useful for horizontal and vertical travel.

For pure vertical distance, use a crouch rocket jump.

For pure horizontal distance, use dash rocket jump.

## Chimera Mk1

By holding down jump, you add fuel to the fuel meter. When this fuel reaches 100%, you activate the active part of Chimera Mk1 and have three seconds until you barrel towards the ground, after which you will be put into burnout until your fuel meter empties.

Chimera Mk1 disables your double jump unless you are put in burnout. This has the additional effect of making edgeboosts less punishing.

While you are flying: You can air dash using the dash button in any cardinal direction infinitely. Dashing costs anywhere from 2-5% fuel.

You can dash jump using the dash button then the jump button in any cardinal direction. Dash distance is cut to 10%. The jump part does not cost any fuel.

You have a maximum velocity of 380 u/s upwards and ~440 u/s in the cardinal directions, achieved over a time span of two seconds.

Chimera Mk1 cools down at variable intervals of 2-5. While you are cursed you are still able to fly.

When you barrel down, you have a smaller collision capsule.
