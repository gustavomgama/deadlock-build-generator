- [Dynamo](Dynamo.md) Hope I reassemble… Teleporters can be used to quickly traverse the map horizontally. There are currently 2 pairs of teleporters on the [Map](Map.md), next to the underground [Secret Shops](Secret Shops.md), which are available to use from the beginning of the match. Teleporters take four seconds to channel after entering the booth, and a [Hero](Hero.md) will gain a small speed boost after teleporting.

Teleporter locations appear on the [Minimap](Minimap.md) as an icon of a person stepping through a portal, facing in the direction of the corresponding exit.

## Notes

[Soul Urn](Soul Urn.md) couriers cannot use teleporters or [Ziplines](Ziplines.md) when delivering the urn.
