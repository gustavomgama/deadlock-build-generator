Pure Damage is a damage type from [Abilities](Ability.md) and [Items](Item.md) that isn't considered [Spirit](Spirit Damage.md), [Weapon](Weapon Damage.md), or [Melee Damage](Melee Damage.md), and thus cannot be mitigated by [Damage Resistance](Damage Resistance.md) or [Barriers](Damage Barrier.md).

## Overview

Pure Damage appears as white numbers, and a grey bar in the damage report tab.

Some items can function similarly to Pure Damage by bypassing mitigation of their damage type.

Some abilities or items show as Pure Damage (white numbers), however they are a post damage mitigation [damage amp](Damage Amplification.md), therefore the damage is already reduced once.

- Venator's Ira Domini and Gutshot (T3) are Blessed, a unique modifier of Pure Damage that can also execute enemies under Ira Domini ExecuteThreshold % health.

## Notes

Pure Damage does not trigger Mystic Reverb. If an ability has bonus Pure Damage like the blessed stakes of Venator's Ira Domini, it will not be used to calculate the damage of the reverb.

Pure Damage was added in the [Six New Heroes](Six New Heroes.md) update. Before this, the abilities and items listed dealt Spirit Damage instead.

Their behavior was similar, bypassing resistances, however some interactions were different, for example Lady Geist's Soul Exchange worked with Mystic Reverb.
