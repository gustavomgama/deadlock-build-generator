The Arroyos are Ivy's surrogate family that live in [Spanish Harlem](wikipedia_East_Harlem.md).

## Summary

20 years before the ritual takes place, the Arroyos were a small family that lived in Spanish Harlem while it was under [Mendoza](The Mendoza Syndicate.md)'s control. One day, Sophia Arroyo and one of her parents were attacked in the streets by thugs, but were saved by Ivy, who was then adopted by the Arroyos. For the next 18 years, Ivy spent her life fighting against the Mendoza Syndicate to free Spanish Harlem for the Arroyos.

In the current day, the Arroyos live happily with Ivy in a Mendoza-free Spanish Harlem. Carlos and Maria had a son named "Manny", while Sophia was heading off for college. Ivy thinks the Arroyos are the best family in the world.

Ivy is reluctant to leave the Arroyos on their own for too long. This could be due to the fear that the Arroyos will be hurt if she's away from them.

The Arroyos eat [Mofongo](wikipedia_Mofongo.md), a traditional Puerto-Rican dish.

The Arroyos are likely Catholic, based on Ivy's reaction to killing Venator.

There are four known Arroyos: Carlos, Maria, Sophia and Manny.

1. Carlos Arroyo Carlos Arroyo is the father of the family.

Carlos works as a first-grade teacher and a community organiser for Spanish Harlem, however he's also running for mayor of New York. In one pathway in [Ivy's visual novel](Ivy's visual novel_Transcription.md), Ivy supports Carlos' political campaign and participates in the ritual to help him become mayor.

- Infernus is voting for Carlos to become mayor. [Billy](Billy.md) thinks Carlos is a fraud who uses Ivy for publicity and to increase the property value of Spanish Harlem.

Carlos is worried about Ivy participating in the ritual. He dislikes [Fairfax Industries](Fairfax Industries.md) and isn't thrilled about Ivy fighting against the [OSIC](Occult Security and Investigation Commission.md) during the ritual.

Ivy idolises Carlos. Carlos tells Ivy that it takes more muscles to frown than to smile. He also says that "you can make opportunities, but you can't make people take them".

1. Maria Arroyo Maria Arroyo is the mother of the family.

Not much is known about Maria, however she does have a date planned with Carlos at the [Baroness Hotel](Baroness Hotel.md). Ivy also doesn't want her to know about Venator's involvement in the ritual.

1. Sophia Arroyo Sophia Arroyo, also known as Sophie, is the elder daughter of the Arroyos. Based on her being a child when Ivy was brought to life, Sophia's age is likely in her mid-twenties.

Unknown to Ivy and the other Arroyos, Sophia works at Wraith's casino, helping Wraith as her bookmaker. In one pathway in [Ivy's visual novel](Ivy's visual novel_Transcription.md), Ivy discovers Sophia's secret job. While the ending of that pathway is unknown, it likely results in Ivy participating in the ritual to keep Sophia safe.

Sophia attends Columbia College as one of Dynamo's students. Sophia speaks highly of Dynamo and Dynamo thinks she's a great student, but he is worried about her due to Sophia sleeping during lessons due to working long shifts at her job. Dynamo is unaware that Sophia works for Wraith.

Sophia tells Ivy that, if she gets mad, she should hold her breath and count to four.

Sophia was the Arroyo who named Ivy.

Sophia listens to Cadence's band.

1. Manny Arroyo Manny Arroyo is the younger son of the Arroyos. Manny's age is unknown, however he's implied to still be in school.

Manny seems to be a bookworm, as he loves reading about Kelvin's adventures and gets taken to libraries by Ivy for Paige's Story Time.
