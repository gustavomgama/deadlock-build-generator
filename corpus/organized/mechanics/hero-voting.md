Hero Voting is a system in [Deadlock](Deadlock.md) where players vote for the order in which new heroes are released during a major [update](update.md).

## Overview

1. Six New Heroes The [Six New Heroes](Six New Heroes.md) update introduced the hero voting system. Players received 1 vote per game played. Votes were cast in the newly-released [Hideout](Hideout.md), and players received a generic sticker in the Hideout's wall with a visual pun on "I voted" for each vote.

Players were supposed to vote based only on each hero's select render and tags, with no gameplay information. However, their voice lines and interactions were shipped early in the game files, giving the community an early look at their personality and playstyle.

- Mina was released first, without the hero vote.

1. Old Gods, New Blood The [Old Gods, New Blood](Old Gods, New Blood.md) update added a bonus of 4 votes for the first win of the day. It also added hero campaign posters as a global cosmetic [item](Items.md), and hero-specific stickers. The posters change their image once damaged.

This time, the hero's voice lines were not shipped until their release, but players found the unreleased captions text file with transcriptions of some of their voice lines and interactions.

1. City Never Sleeps The [City Never Sleeps](City Never Sleeps.md) update added a voting booth in the pre-game lobby. Players vote on their hero in the pre-game phase, with their chosen hero appearing under the player's portrait in the top bar. The winning team's votes are doubled, and players also receive double votes on the first win of the day. The old voting terminal in the Hideout now only shows the player's vote count.

This time, little information on the new heroes was added or datamined ahead of time. Each hero received a few voice lines that played when they were voted for, giving a hint of their personality.
