Sinner's Sacrifice machines are interactable objects on the [map](map.md) that may be broken using [melee attacks](Melee Attack.md) to earn [Souls](Souls.md) and [permanent buffs](permanent buffs.md).

## Overview

Sinner's Sacrifice machines first spawn at `/60` minutes into the game, and respawn `/60` minutes after being broken (when at a location by themselves) or once a "hybrid camp" is fully cleared. A "hybrid camp" consists of 1 or more Sinner's Sacrifice, and 1 or more [Haunts](Haunts.md). In order to clear a hybrid camp, all Sinner's Sacrifices must be broken, and all Haunts killed.

There are a total of 15 Sinner's Sacrifices across 11 locations, appearing in 4 different configurations on the map.

6 locations of a Sinner's Sacrifice by itself.

2 hybrid camp locations of 1 Sinner's Sacrifice, and 2 [Medium Haunts](Haunts.md).

2 hybrid camp locations of 2 Sinner's Sacrifices, and 1 [Medium Haunt](Haunts.md).

1 location of 3 Sinner's Sacrifices.

Locations with Sinner's Sacrifices are shown on the map with a icon.

It should be noted that the icon represents the site and not necessarily the Sinner's Sacrifice machine itself, depending on that location's configuration. While [Crates](Crates.md) and [Golden Statue](Golden Statue.md)s may appear at these locations, they are not part of them and do not need to be destroyed in order to clear and start the respawn timer for that location.

## Behaviour

The machine spawns in [attract mode](Wikipedia_Glossary_of_video_game_terms.md#attract-mode) and plays music until it is activated.

Like [Haunts](Haunts.md), the Sinner's Sacrifice's remaining payout value is shown above the machine.

1. Interacting with the machine Each machine has 500 health and can only be damaged by [melee attacks](Melee Attack.md). A light melee deals 50 damage, and a heavy melee deals 100 damage.

The machine retaliates against attackers by dealing

- neutral_sinners_sacrifice RetaliateDamage damage per melee hit, with the minimum retaliation damage to a hero being 320, and a maximum of 640, depending on the mixture of weights of melee strikes: completing a machine with only light melee damage takes 8 hits, whereas heavy melees only take 4 hits. There is virtually no time difference between completing a Sinner's Sacrifice exclusively with light or heavy melee attacks, with the exception of - Billy, who has faster light melee recovery and thus can complete a machine faster than with heavy melees.

The machine's health is indicated by the lightbulbs at the top of it. The first melee attack will cause all the lights to turn on, and further hits will cause lights to turn off from right to left. A light melee will turn off one bulb per hit and a heavy melee turns off two bulbs. The last two bulbs will always turn off together.

After the Sinner's Sacrifice has been hit at least once, the machine will not only play a ticking sound at low volume until it is hit again or it enters the Jackpot mode.

1. Jackpot After being reduced to three or less bulbs (300 damage taken), the machine picks between two speeds for its Jackpot Mode, a looping timing minigame. The minigame has a `*100`% chance to be fast and `*100`% to be slow. The speed chosen is indicated by the remaining lights: Static and white for slow, and Blinking between orange and red for fast.

After being reduced to one or zero bulbs (400 damage taken), the Sinner's Sacrifice will enter the Jackpot Mode. The lights will begin turning on from left to right accompanied by a count of 8 sound effects at the random speed.

Hitting the machine while all the bulbs are turned on will result in a Jackpot, represented by all bulbs briefly blinking before its destruction and a special tune. The final hit awards the remaining souls and a different number of [permanent buffs](permanent buffs.md) depending on the melee type; hitting a jackpot with a heavy melee awards 4 random buffs, whereas a light melee only awards 1 random buff. Missing the timing completely only awards the remaining souls.

The machine deals no retaliation damage during this state.

1. Hero Specific Interactions Abilities that deal melee damage, such as - Viscous ' Puddle Punch or - Calico's Leaping Slash, will not affect the machine.

Despite dealing spirit damage, the AoE of - Baba's Granny Long Legs heavy melee will hit Sinner's Sacrifice, allowing her to farm multiple machines at once.

- Rem's Lil Helpers can be assigned to Sinner's Sacrifice machines, and will always secure full jackpots as if done with a heavy melee. They get interrupted and return to Rem if a player hits the machine.

Helpers wait until the final lightbulbs start flashing to do the last hit, allowing a short window of time for another player to get the jackpot first.

- Pocket with - Barrage stacks can deal more damage per hit to the Sinner's. This is likely a bug.

## Notable Locations

The Bell Tower in the [Chinatown District](Chinatown District.md) (Between the Blue and Green lanes on the Hidden King's side) features three Sinner's Sacrifices at the top. Hitting the machines, or any of the [breakables](breakables.md) there, will cause the bell to ring, which can be heard by all players.

The Sunken Plaza in the [Plaza District](Plaza District.md) (Between the Blue and Yellow lanes on the Archmother's side) features two sites of Sinner's Sacrifice machines alongside two camps of [Haunts](Haunts.md). The fog covering the area obscures all sight and sound, but slowly drains players' stamina.

## Rewards

1. Souls A Sinner's Sacrifice's soul value increases over the course of a match. This value starts at - * 2 and grows by - * / 100 * 2 round 2 per minute from the beginning of the match.

- sinners: 1 This total value is distributed in [Haunted Souls](Souls.md#Haunted-Souls) with each hit: A light melee grants the total value divided by 20, rounded.

A heavy melee grants the total value divided by 10, rounded.

The final hit grants the total value divided by 2 (rounded), plus one heavy melee payout.

The payout is intended to grant half of its souls from melee attacks and the other half when destroyed. However, due to rounding on each hit, the total souls granted may be slightly more or less than the internal value shown above. If multiple people hit the machine, subsequent melee attacks will attempt to evenly distribute the payout to everyone that has hit it.

1. Buffs Successfully hitting the Jackpot provides the player with stacks of [permanent buffs](permanent buffs.md).

Hitting the jackpot with a light melee gives the player 1 buff.

Hitting with a heavy melee gives 4 buffs.
