Permanent Buffs, commonly called [Golden Statue](Golden Statue.md) Buffs, are small, permanent positive stat modifiers that can be acquired through various sources over the course of a match.

## Overview

Permanent buffs are obtained as the player farms/traverses the various [Districts](The Cursed Apple.md#Districts) between the Lanes, upon completing certain Objectives, or from specific items. Upon collection, they will give a slight increase to one of 10 possible [stats](stats.md), selected at random, which are listed below. While the individual value of one of these buffs is fairly inconsequential, as their name would imply, they are truly permanent stat increases that never expire and persist for the remainder of the game, even through death, similar to the rewards from [Boons](Boon.md), meaning they can make a surprisingly big difference as a player collects them throughout a match.

The stat values of permanent buffs have 3 "levels" based on how much time has passed when the buff is received, becoming more potent as a match goes on. Buffs start at Level 1, Level 2 buffs can be obtained after 10 minutes have passed, and Level 3 buffs can be obtained after 30 minutes have passed.

Stats gained from permanent buffs can be seen by hovering over the [Weapon](Stats.md#Weapon), [Vitality](Stats.md#Vitality), or [Spirit](Stats.md#Spirit) stats in the bottom left of the player's UI and then mousing over the appropriate stat.

## List of Buffs

For [Golden Statue](Golden Statue.md)s specifically, the drop rate only considers which buffs were spawned by the player breaking statues, even if they weren't picked up (meaning that a player can't manipulate the drop rate by not picking up specific buffs).
