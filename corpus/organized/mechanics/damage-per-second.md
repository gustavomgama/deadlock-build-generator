- size: 32, or DPS, is a function of a hero's [Bullet Damage](Bullet Damage.md) and [Fire Rate](Fire Rate.md). It refers to the amount of damage per second the hero's weapons will deal if continuously fired, and is calcuated by the amount of bullets shot per second multiplied by the bullet damage.
