Rope is a climbable object in the game that can be grabbed onto by pressing the jump key (Space by default). If a player is hit by a [melee attack](melee attack.md) while on a rope, they are [slowed](Movement Slow.md) by 80%, which ramps down to 20% over 2s.

If a [Hero](Hero.md) has taken damage from another Hero in the last 4s, they will climb ropes 40% slower, which tapers off to 0% in the last second.
