The Paradox Organization is a guild of thieves who want to show the world that there's nothing they can't steal.

## Summary

The Paradox Organization, also known as Paradox for short, is a group of bored rich thieves who steal various objects of interest. Whatever Paradox steals is presented at the [Paradox Museum](Paradox Museum.md), a pop-up gallery open to the public with the intention of showing the world that Paradox can steal from anyone.

The Paradox Organization does not exclusively steal artifacts: they also steal state secrets and blackmail on celebrities.

- Paradox (the playable character) is one member of the Paradox Organization. Each member of Paradox wears a mask and a coat, and gives up their name to join the organization.

The members of Paradox work mostly independently, gathering together a few times a year to discuss their thefts. The thefts of each Paradox member is "based on individual interest and challenge".

The Paradox Organization has a council. It's implied by - The Doorman that Paradox is afraid of being seen as a failure by the council.

The origins of the Paradox Organization are unknown. Their oldest known theft seems to be one of [Johann Geist's](Johann Geist.md) paintings, which happened 50 years before the events of the game.
