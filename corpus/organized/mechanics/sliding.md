- inputs: Crouch walk above 350 u/s or when on a ramp Sliding is a fundamental part of movement in [Deadlock](Deadlock.md). Sliding allows you to gain momentum down slopes and to conserve already gained momentum to gain further distance. Sliding is only available while on the ground. While sliding, a blue trail (green if playing as - Vyper ) will be emitted behind you, with small dust particles on your sides. One unique property of sliding is that while sliding, you have infinite ammunition; this is incredibly useful for combat and maximising DPS Damage per second.

Sliding takes no [stamina](stamina.md) to execute; you only have to be moving at a speed higher than 350 u/s (or 8.9 m/s) to initiate the slide. After executing the slide, you will eventually decelerate to a complete stopSliding has a friction coefficient of 0.1875. Cancelling the slide before you reach speeds slower than walking by releasing the duck button is advised.

Sliding for more than 0.75 seconds (or 1.00 second when [Dash Jumping](Dash Jumping.md)) applies increased frictionThe late slide friction penalty sets friction coefficient to around 3.1875 when sliding from a grounded dash, and 0.6875 when from a Dash Jump. Contibution by Altimor.. This impacts your ability to maintain momentum. Items with the +Slide Distance statistic increase the time until the increased friction is applied by increments of +0.25 seconds.

To execute a slide, move above the required speed threshold (350 u/s or 8.9 m/s) or be moving at any speed on a ramp, and crouch. You should automatically slide.

- name: Slide Storage

- file: slideconservation.mp4

- tech_type: Universal Tech

- difficulty: Basic Mechanics

- stamina: 0

- character_dependent: No

- inputs: Crouch walk above 350 u/s or when on a ramp 1. Slide Storage Slide Storage Also known as Slide Conservation is the act of infinitely buffering a slide input for later use. This allows you to slide on objects and ramps instantly without minimal speed. Incredibly small parts of map geometry (e.g., curbs, lips of edges) that would be impossible to slide on normally can be slid on using Slide Storage.

Slide Storage allows you to buffer a slide input while in the air; this allows you to slide after any airborne action (e.g., dash jumping, jumping).

To execute, be airborne (through [jumping](jumping.md), dash jumping, etc.) and then hold the crouch button. While the crouch button is held, your slide is being buffered. If you then move down a ramp, fall off a slope, or have enough speed, you will begin sliding.

1. Crouch Storage By applying the inverse principles of Slide Storage, one can instead trigger Crouch Storage Also known as Crouch Conservation. This allows you to stop on objects and ramps nearly instantly without sliding.

Crouch Storage allows you to hold a crouch while in the air; this allows you to stay crouching after any airborne action (with the exception of down dashing).

To execute, hold the crouch button before dashing or going airborne. While the crouch button is held, your crouch is stored. Now, no matter what surface you're on or what speed you're moving, you will never slide.
