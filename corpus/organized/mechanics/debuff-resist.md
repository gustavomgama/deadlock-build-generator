[Debuff Resist](Debuff Resist.md) is a [stat](Stats.md) which reduces the length of time [negative effects](Status Effects.md) affect a [hero](Hero.md).

This stat is gained in the form of a percentage. As an example, "30% Debuff Resist" will reduce the duration of a 10 second debuff by 3 seconds.

## Interactions with Crowd-Control

Debuff Resist reduces the duration of stuns and similar effects inflicted by the following abilities: This effect helps mitigate crowd-control by shortening stuns' durations, allowing the hero to recover more quickly.
