## Overview

Most crates spawn after minutes has elapsed in the match and usually respawn minutes after being broken, though sometimes the respawn may be delayed. However, the crates in the room below [Times Squares](The Cursed Apple.md#Broadway), directly above the [Midboss](Midboss.md) pit spawn after minutes and take minutes to respawn. Crates in the [Tunnels](Tunnel.md) take minutes to spawn and to respawn.

Souls provided from crates are Haunted Souls. Not collecting the souls and leaving them on the ground will delay the respawn if it isn't collected within the normal respawn time.

Souls collected from crates are tracked in the post-game "Souls by Source" graph as "Breakable Pickups".

## Soul Values

- crates: 1

## Notes

Even though crates spawn after 3 in-game minutes, their soul scaling starts immediately.

There are 518 crates on the map.
