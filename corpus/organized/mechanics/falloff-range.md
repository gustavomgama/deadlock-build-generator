Falloff Range is a [stat](Stats.md) that determines the falloff of a [Hero's](Hero.md) [Weapon Damage](Weapon Damage.md) over distance.

## Overview

For most heroes, weapon damage falls off by -90% at maximum range and beyond, following a linear scale from minimum range. See "Base Falloff Range" table below for exceptions.

Besides Weapon Damage, falloff range also affects the damage of some weapon-related [abilities](abilities.md) and [items](items.md), most notably [Build-Up](Build-Up.md) effects.

Falloff range can be increased with items.

The following heroes have exceptions to falloff range: Apollo's weapon has no damage falloff and deals no damage beyond its max range.

- Bebop's weapon has a separate Max Range stat that increases with [Boons](Boon.md), and deals no damage beyond its max range.

- Drifter's weapon deals no damage beyond its max range.

- Graves ' weapon has no damage falloff and deals no damage beyond its max range.

Despite having no damage falloff, the falloff range is factored in when calculating Graves' [Build-Up](Build-Up.md) per bullet, most notably the buld-up from Essence Theft.

As an exception, the effect from Ricochet has damage falloff, calculated based on the distance from the main target to the ricochet'd target.

- Silver (Transformed)'s weapon has a fixed range that cannot be increased.

1. Explosive bullets Alternate fire weapons with explosive bullets deal 50% damage at maximum explosion radius, multiplied by the regular damage falloff.
