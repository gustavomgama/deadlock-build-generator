Stats are divided into three categories: Weapon, Vitality, and Spirit. Stats can be modified by purchasing [items](items.md), [leveling up](Level.md), gaining buffs, or through [scaling](scaling.md) (most commonly with [spirit power](spirit power.md)).

See [Hero Comparison Table](Hero Comparison Table.md) for a full list and comparison of all hero stats.
