- /60 Buff Containers, also known as "Golden Statues" or just "Statues", are [breakable](breakables.md) objects scattered around the map that, when broken, have a chance to drop [permanent stat buffs](Permanent Buff.md). Buff Containers can be broken by punching them, using certain abilities, shooting them, or by dashing, sliding or even just walking through them, provided you have enough momentum.

## Overview

Most Buff Containers spawn after minutes has elapsed in the match and usually respawn minutes after being broken, though sometimes the respawn may be delayed. However, the containers in the room below [Times Square](The Cursed Apple.md#Broadway), directly above the [Midboss](Midboss.md) pit, spawn after minutes and take minutes to respawn. Buff Containers in the [Tunnels](Tunnel.md) take minutes to spawn and to respawn. Not collecting the buff and leaving it on the ground will delay the respawn if it isn't collected within the normal respawn time.

When destroyed, they have a 50% chance to drop a random booklet providing a stat buff. All buffs obtained are permanent when picked up.

## Buffs Granted

For Buff Containers, the drop rate only considers which buffs were spawned by the player breaking statues, even if they weren't picked up (meaning that a player can't manipulate the drop rate by not picking up specific buffs).
