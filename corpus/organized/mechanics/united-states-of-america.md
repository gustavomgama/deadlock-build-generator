The United States of America is a country on Earth in which the [Cursed Apple](Cursed Apple.md) is located.

## Description

The United States is composed of 52 states. The 50th State is the Island of Cadenza, the 51st is Puerto Rico, and the 52nd is [South Ixia](Ixia.md), which joined the Union 5 years before the [ritual](ritual.md). Other known states include New York, Wyoming, Massachusetts, Illinois, Pennsylvania and Wisconsin.

There are at least 2 known [Astral Gates](Astral Gates.md) located in US territory: New York and Puerto Rico.

The [OSIC](Occult Security and Investigation Commission.md) is an organization within the US government.

The US government is sending aid to [Ixia](Ixia.md) as part of their military campaign, drawing opposition from the [Friends of Humanity](Friends of Humanity.md).

The US government is negotiating a sale of a part of Wyoming with the [Djinn](Mirage.md).
