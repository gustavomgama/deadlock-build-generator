Seasonal events are temporary events in [Deadlock](Deadlock.md) to celebrate holidays.

## Halloween

The 2024 Halloween event ran from October 31 to November 7. The 2025 Halloween event ran from October 24 to November 12.

It added 4 Halloween masks for heroes and pumpkin models for boxes, statues and [Denizens](Denizens.md). The Halloween masks were randomized.

## Winter

1. 2025 The 2025 Winter event ran from December 15 to January 20.

During the event, all players received a unique [Snowball](Snowball.md) item that could be leveled up by winning matches with different Heroes, which also unlocked ornaments for the Christmas tree in the [Hideout](Hideout.md). Players who participated in the Winter challenge also unlocked a souvenir above their fireplace in the [Hideout](Hideout.md).

The [Shopkeeper](Shopkeeper.md) also received new holiday-themed lines for all heroes, and [Drifter](Drifter.md) has holiday-themed songs when using Eternal Night.

1. 2024 The 2024 Winter event ran from December 17 to January 17.

It added a new main menu animation, remixed music for the menu and shops, and Winter-themed decorations in the streets, buildings, and [Curiosity Shop](Curiosity Shop.md). The [Shopkeeper](Shopkeeper.md) featured a Santa mask and unique lines. In addition, the [Denizens](Denizen.md) were modelled after snowmen. [Dynamo](Dynamo.md) and the [Newscaster](Newscaster.md) had unique holiday voice lines.

All the mainline [Heroes](Heroes.md) received unique Winter skins, which could be unlocked by completing their respective challenge. A list of which skins were unlocked during the event can be seen in the "Showcase" tab in the player's profile, next to the "Match History" button. Players who participated in the Winter challenge also unlocked a snow globe above their fireplace in the [Hideout](Hideout.md).

1. Skin challenges As the winter skin challenges weren't listed in-game or in patch notes, the challenges were discovered and shared by the player community over the duration of the event.

These challenges could be achieved only in a normal match or a labs match, not in a custom game or against bots. The conditions for each challenge must be completed in one match.

Winter skins were tracked in-game by a Santa hat icon in the main menu that showed how many skins had already been unlocked. Heroes with Winter skins had the Santa hat icon and a red background in the Heroes page.
