- inputs: Move (WASD) Walking is the most common movement action in [Deadlock](Deadlock.md). Walking allows your character to navigate around the [Cursed Apple](Cursed Apple.md).

When you start walking, you will begin to start accelerating towards a peak speed which is dependent on character, which can be increased with items. When you stop walking, you will gradually decelerate to a complete stop.

You can also walk while crouching. When crouch walking, you will move at a speed of 4.75 m/s.

1. Characters Whose Move Speed Increases with Spirit Power Certain characters have move speed that scales with [Spirit Power](Spirit Power.md) (indicated by the symbol). Move speed increases by a fixed coefficient that varies by character.
