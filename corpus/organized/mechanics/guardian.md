Guardians is the other type of defence other than the M Walkers present in the lanes. Comperable to Towers in other MOBA's, they are static in their location, and need to be destroyed to progress further into the lane to reach the M Patron.

## Walker Locations

There are a total of twelve Guardians on the Map, with a total of three in each lane. One being the first structure in the lane, followed by a M Walker in the lane, you will find a duo of Guardians as the last structure in the lane in order to reach the enemies base where you can destroy the Shrines.

## Guardian Combat

Walkers are immune to damage until a M Trooper or Hero (from it's opposing team) is nearby. Once vulnerable you can damage it. The first Lane Guardian has a total of 5,500 health, while the Base Guardians have 4,300 health each. At the start of the game the guardians take 70% reduced damage from M Troopers. This reduces by 10% every minute, and eventually caps at taking an increased +30% Damage (from Troopers) at 10 minutes. To attack the Lane Guardians you also need to be within a 20 meter range.

The Walkers attack Heroes and Troopers through a Fire Beam that targets a the closest target.

1. Base Guardians As mentioned, the Base Guardians come in pairs in each lane, compared to the regular Lane Guardian, they have additional resistances and health regeneration.

Once the Base Guardians in a lane are killed, the team that destroyed them recieves a Ziplane base speed boost of a 120% that ramps up over 4 seconds.

1. Bullet Resistance The Guardians near the base have an additional trait, where they initially have 40% Bullet Resistance. This decreases with 20% for each enemy hero present. If all six players are nearby, they have a -80% Bullet Resistance.

## Souls & Ability Points

Guardians contain a total of 275 M Souls (for a player) once a Guardian is destroyed. The M Souls appear as 4 Soul Orbs and will need to be secured. All secured orbs are then awarded to every played on a team If all players on the team are alive, and all souls would be secured, this grants the team a total of 1,650 M Souls.

## Flex Slots

Once thee lane Guardians are destroyed, the first flex slot is unlocked for all players on the team.
