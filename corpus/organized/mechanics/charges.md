[Charges](Charges.md) are stored uses of [Hero](Hero.md) [abilities](Ability.md). When an ability with charges is used, a single charge is consumed. When an ability runs out of charges, it cannot be used again until the next charge is ready.

An ability with charges is displayed with a smaller bar above it. The bar's segments represent the number of charges. A white segment represents a usable charge, a purple partially-filled segment represents a charge that is being restored, and a grey segment represents a fully expended charge.

After using a charge there is a cooldown preventing immediate use of another charge. This cooldown varies by ability and is not affected by [cooldown reduction](Cooldown Reduction.md), but is affected by items with a Faster Time Between Charges effect, such as Rapid Recharge.

## Restoring Charges

As long as an ability does not have its maximum number of charges, it will passively restore charges over time. An ability's [cooldown](cooldown.md) represents the length of time it takes to restore a single charge.

Charges are restored one at a time, not all at once.

Some abilities do not replenish with time and instead use other conditions to gain a charge. For example Wraith's Card Trick gains a charge when she deals damage to enemies.

## Maximum Charges

- Infernus ' Napalm upgrades, the first upgrade increases the number of charges the ability has.](Maximum_Charges_on_Ability_Upgrades.png) Abilities with charges can store up to a maximum number of them. Each ability has its own default maximum amount.

Some abilities have upgrades that can increase the number of maximum charges they have. Additionally, specific [items](items.md) can be purchased which grant additional [Bonus Ability Charges](untitled.md#Bonus-Ability-Charges).

## Bonus Ability Charges

## Notes

[Echo Shard](Echo Shard.md), when imbued on a charged ability, will only restore a single charge. However, [Refresher](Refresher.md) will restore all charges.

Both [Echo Shard](Echo Shard.md) and [Refresher](Refresher.md) additionally reset the cooldown between uses of the ability.
