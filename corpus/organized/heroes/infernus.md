# Infernus

**Tags:** Arsonist · Explosive · Burn Rubber  
**Type:** marksman

## Abilities

### Napalm

**Base Damage:** 40 · **Cooldown:** 25s

Sprays an incendiary mixture in front of you for 40 spirit damage, slowing targets by 28% for 4s and coating them in napalm for 8s. 20m range, with charges.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +1 charge |
| 2 | Napalmed targets give you +15% lifesteal |
| 3 | Napalmed targets take +17% damage and receive 33% less healing |

### Flame Dash

**Base Damage:** 30 · **Cooldown:** 38s

Dash forward at up to 20 m/s with slow resistance, leaving a 4.5m-wide trail of fire that burns enemies (30 DPS) for 4s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -12s cooldown |
| 2 | +1s trail duration and +20 DPS |
| 3 | 2 charges (14s recharge) |

### Afterburn

**Base Damage:** 14

Passive. Your bullets build up a burn that deals spirit damage over time (14 DPS, 3s base). Your abilities refresh the burn's duration.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +16 burn DPS |
| 2 | Burning enemies deal 35% less spirit damage |
| 3 | +3s maximum burn duration |

### Concussive Combustion

**Base Damage:** 125 · **Cooldown:** 190s

Become a living bomb. After a 3.25s delay you explode, dealing 125 spirit damage and stunning enemies within 12m for 1.25s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +100 damage |
| 2 | -65s cooldown and +100% explosion lifesteal |
| 3 | +0.9s stun and +10m radius |
