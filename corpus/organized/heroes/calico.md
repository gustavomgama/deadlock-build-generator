# Calico

**Tags:** Tricksy · Slippery · Burst Damage  
**Type:** assassin

## Weapon

The weapon's spread pattern fires 9 bullets in the shape of a diamond, with one bullet at the center.

## Abilities

### Gloom Bombs

**Base Damage:** 45 · **Cooldown:** 14s

Throw a cluster of 4 bombs that each deal 45 spirit damage in 3m. Enemies hit by several bombs take 65% damage from the extras.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -3s cooldown |
| 2 | Each bomb hit applies -6% melee resist for 6s |
| 3 | +3 bombs |

### Leaping Slash

**Base Damage:** 10 · **Cooldown:** 13s

Dash forward and slash enemies around you for melee damage. Heals you for 40 when you hit a hero.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +25 healing |
| 2 | Heroes you kill within 3s of hitting them give +200 souls |
| 3 | +60 damage; 50% cooldown refund on hero hit |

### Ava

**Cooldown:** 30s

Turn into shadow for 15s, gaining 30–65% move speed (it builds up over 4s and resets if an enemy hero damages you) and disappearing from the minimap.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +15s duration |
| 2 | +15 health regen and +40% max move speed |
| 3 | Builds up to 18% damage amplification, which lingers for 6s after leaving |

### Return to Shadows

**Base Damage:** 150 · **Cooldown:** 115s

Instantly become untargetable, dealing 150 spirit damage around you and gaining move speed. After 3s you return, dealing another 150 in 7.5m.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -20s cooldown |
| 2 | +75 damage and +20% move speed |
| 3 | Heals 450 and refreshes all your other cooldowns |
