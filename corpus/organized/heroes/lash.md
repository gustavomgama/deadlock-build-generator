# Lash

**Tags:** Initiator · High-Flying · Arrogant  
**Type:** assassin

## Abilities

### Ground Strike

**Base Damage:** 60 · **Cooldown:** 18s

Slam the ground, damaging enemies in front of you. If you're in the air, you dive to the ground first, and the attack hits harder the further you fall.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -10s cooldown |
| 2 | Knocks enemies up and slows them by 40% for 3s |
| 3 | +120% damage per meter fallen and better scaling |

### Grapple

**Cooldown:** 35s

Pull yourself towards a target, resetting your air jumps and dashes.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -17s cooldown |
| 2 | +20m cast range and +7 weapon damage for 6s |
| 3 | +1 charge, +1 stamina and +60% air control on cast |

### Flog

**Base Damage:** 65 · **Cooldown:** 26s

Lash enemies with your whip, healing for part of the damage dealt.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | Applies a 28% move slow for 3s |
| 2 | Applies -30% fire rate; -16s cooldown |
| 3 | +80 damage, a wider attack and +15% heal |

### Death Slam

**Base Damage:** 105 · **Cooldown:** 170s

Channel for 2.3s to connect your whips to enemies in front of you, then lift and stun them before slamming them into the ground at a chosen spot.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +12m maximum throw distance |
| 2 | -35s cooldown |
| 3 | Stuns enemies in the landing radius for 1.2s; +6m cast range |
