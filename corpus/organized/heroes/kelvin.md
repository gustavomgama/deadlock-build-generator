# Kelvin

**Tags:** Protector · Explorer · Ice Cold  
**Type:** brawler

## Abilities

### Frost Grenade

**Base Damage:** 60 · **Cooldown:** 30s

Throw a grenade that damages enemies and heals allies in a 6.5m radius, slowing enemies by 32% for 4s. 2 charges.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +30 damage and +30 healing |
| 2 | -10s cooldown; also freezes stamina regen |
| 3 | +2m radius and better spirit scaling |

### Ice Path

**Cooldown:** 50s

Create a trail of ice for 8s that gives allies +2 m/s move and sprint speed. Kelvin gets 60% slow resistance.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +2 m/s move speed and +35% bullet resist for Kelvin on the path |
| 2 | -25s cooldown |
| 3 | +35% spirit resist and +20 spirit power for Kelvin on the path |

### Arctic Beam

**Base Damage:** 45 · **Cooldown:** 28s

Channel a freezing beam for 5s (45 DPS) that steadily slows the target's movement (up to 16%) and fire rate (up to 20%).

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | An extra -20% fire rate and move speed |
| 2 | +20 DPS and better spirit scaling |
| 3 | -13s cooldown; fires 2 extra beams at enemies within 10m of your target |

### Frozen Shelter

**Cooldown:** 185s

Create a dome for 5s that heals allies inside (90 health per second) and slows enemies by 28%.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -20s cooldown |
| 2 | +1.5s duration |
| 3 | Purges non-ultimate debuffs; +65 health regen |
