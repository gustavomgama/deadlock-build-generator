# Pocket

**Tags:** Trickster · Burst Damage · Frogs  
**Type:** assassin

## Weapon

The weapon's spread pattern fires 7 bullets in the shape of a hexagon with a flat top, with one bullet at the center.

## Abilities

### Barrage

**Cooldown:** 32s

Channel for 2s, launching projectiles that deal spirit damage and slow. Hitting heroes builds stacking damage amplification.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +16 damage per projectile |
| 2 | -16s cooldown |
| 3 | +4% amplification per stack and +3m radius |

### Flying Cloak

**Base Damage:** 60 · **Cooldown:** 26s

Launch a sentient cloak that deals spirit damage. Press again to teleport to wherever the cloak is.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +70 damage |
| 2 | +5 weapon damage for 6s |
| 3 | +1.6s cloak lifetime and -10s cooldown |

### Enchanter's Satchel

**Base Damage:** 65 · **Cooldown:** 17s

Hide inside your suitcase, then burst out to deal spirit damage to nearby enemies.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -5s cooldown |
| 2 | +90 damage |
| 3 | +1.5s duration and +4m radius; enemies hit get -32% move speed and fire rate for 4s |

### Affliction

**Base Damage:** 32 · **Cooldown:** 170s

Afflict nearby enemies with damage over time. Affliction can't kill on its own and doesn't trigger item effects.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -35s cooldown |
| 2 | +3s duration and +4m radius |
| 3 | Blocks all healing on afflicted enemies; +13 DPS and better spirit scaling |
