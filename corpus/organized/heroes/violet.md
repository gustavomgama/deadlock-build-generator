# Violet

**Tags:** Ambitious · Nimble · Creative  
**Type:** mystic

## Abilities

### Living Ink

**Base Damage:** 35 · **Cooldown:** 22s

Two charges. Press it to empower your next 3 bullets for 10s. You can steer them in flight with your aim, they deal 20% more bullet damage plus 35 spirit damage in a small 2.5m splash, and every hero hit takes 1.5s off all your ability cooldowns (and half that off your items). Its alt-fire, Draw, instead paints a solid ink stroke up to 25m long that blocks heroes and projectiles for 20s. A heavy melee breaks the stroke, but the attacker takes spirit damage for it.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -4s cooldown and +15m drawing length |
| 2 | Each empower gives +10% fire rate for 10s, stacking up to 5 times |
| 3 | Hero hits take 2.5s off cooldowns (up from 1.5s), +30% bullet damage on empowered shots and better spirit scaling |

### Flow State

**Base Damage:** 100 · **Cooldown:** 23s

Send a brush gliding forward. It leaves a trail of ink for 6s, and allies who slide along the trail slide much farther (+100%). Enemies it passes through take 50 to 100 spirit damage, more the farther it has travelled (full damage after 2s), and a 60% slow that fades over 2s. Press the ability again to flick the brush forward, ending it early for spirit damage in a larger area.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | Sliding gives +150% stamina regeneration |
| 2 | -6s cooldown, +8m flick range and +4s trail duration |
| 3 | Sliding builds up to +20% damage amp, which lasts 6s |

### Still Life

**Base Damage:** 60 · **Cooldown:** 35s

Pick a hero within 25m as your subject and start a 2.6s sketch. While you keep them in front of you, they take 30 spirit damage per second, and your shots and ability hits on them fill the sketch faster. If you finish it, they take another 60 spirit damage, are stunned for 1s and drop a sketch. Pick it up for a permanent stat bonus that depends on the hero you drew (weapon damage, spirit power, max health or move speed).

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | Casting gives 15 health regen for 4s, refreshed when you pick up a sketch |
| 2 | Your subject deals 35% less damage while you draw them |
| 3 | +20% move speed, -11s cooldown and +0.35s stun |

### Abstract Landscape

**Base Damage:** 100 · **Cooldown:** 165s

Paint a huge ring of ink around you (18m radius) for 6s. It blocks vision, and allies inside get +25 spirit power. Enemies inside are revealed, and any enemy who leaves takes 100 spirit damage, is silenced for 2s and slowed by 30%, which also destroys the ink.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -20s cooldown |
| 2 | Allies inside also get +25% spirit lifesteal and +15 more spirit power |
| 3 | +1.25s silence, +4s duration, and the ink is no longer destroyed when enemies leave |
