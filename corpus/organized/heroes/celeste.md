# Celeste

**Tags:** Performer · Disruptive · Dazzling  
**Type:** mystic

## Weapon

Carny Life's projectile flies in an arc and bounces on the first surface it hits. If there's an enemy in range at a narrow angle (~45º spread) from the shot, the projectile homes into them / flies in their direction. It will also home into [soul orbs](Soul Orbs.md). If shot directly at an enemy, the projectile can ricochet to a second enemy at close distance and aligned at a narrow angle. The ricocheted shot can headshot the second enemy, if shot at a high enough angle to hit their head, and it will trigger headshot item effects. Split Shot generates 3 multishots at 70° spread.

## Abilities

### Light Eater

**Base Damage:** 15 · **Cooldown:** 20s

Blast enemies in a 10m cone for 40 spirit damage, marking them for 8s so they take more spirit damage. You get 18% spirit lifesteal against marked enemies.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +15% spirit lifesteal |
| 2 | -10s cooldown and +3m range |
| 3 | +22 damage and better spirit scaling |

### Dazzling Trick

**Cooldown:** 38s

Gain a 100-point barrier for 4s. When it breaks, it silences enemies within 14m for 1.75s and deals 50% of the barrier as damage.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +3.5 m/s move speed |
| 2 | +70 barrier and better scaling |
| 3 | +1.25s silence, -20s cooldown; Shining Wonder can bounce off you |

### Radiant Daggers

**Base Damage:** 55 · **Cooldown:** 33s

Call down a beam of light (30m range) dealing 55 spirit damage in 8m. Each hit gives you a stack (up to 6, lasting 30s) of +7% spirit amp and extra fire rate.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +2 charges |
| 2 | -22s cooldown and +70 damage |
| 3 | +9% fire rate and +4% spirit amp per stack |

### Shining Wonder

**Base Damage:** 140 · **Cooldown:** 160s

Launch an orb that deals 140 spirit damage, slows movement by 32% and dash distance by 22% for 1.5s, then bounces to up to 8 targets within 15.5m (heroes first). It lingers for 3.25s looking for targets.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | An extra -16% move slow and -14% dash distance |
| 2 | +90 damage and better spirit scaling |
| 3 | +6 bounces and -30s cooldown |
