# Shiv

**Tags:** Rage · Bleed · Repeat  
**Type:** brawler

## Weapon

Busted Flush has an alternate fire mode, which has a knockback recoil that can be used for movement. The knockback can be removed by sources of [Movement Silence](Movement Silence.md), such as Slowing Hex. The primary fire's spread pattern shoots six bullets in the shape of an S with a tight spread, with one bullet at the center and two bullets closely clustered in the bottom right. The alt fire's spread pattern shoots 12 bullets mostly in the shape of a diamond. Besides the centermost bullet and the 8 outermost bullets in a diamond shape, there are two extra bullets fired just to the bottom right of the top left bullet and to the top left of the bottom right bullet. Lastly, there is one additional bullet fired just above halfway between the center and bottom bullets. Alt-fire stats: Takes ammo rounds per shot. ## Abilities

### Serrated Knives

**Cooldown:** 16s

Throw a knife that makes the target bleed for 10 DPS over 5s. 2 charges. At full rage, knives also deal 3.5% of current health on impact and slow by 28%.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +2s bleed duration |
| 2 | +2 charges |
| 3 | +12 bleed DPS and better spirit scaling |

### Slice and Dice

**Base Damage:** 75 · **Cooldown:** 16s

Dash 12m forward, dealing 75 spirit damage and reducing spirit resist by 6%. At full rage, an echo retraces the dash after a short delay.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -6s cooldown |
| 2 | An extra -4% spirit resist and +2m range |
| 3 | +50 impact damage; -2s cooldown per hero hit |

### Bloodletting

**Cooldown:** 25s

Passive: 25% of incoming damage is deferred and taken over 6s instead. Activate to clear 30% of your deferred damage. At full rage, you defer 15% more.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -15s cooldown |
| 2 | Clears +40% more deferred damage |
| 3 | Defers +15% more damage |

### Killing Blow

**Base Damage:** 200 · **Cooldown:** 145s

Leap at an enemy (12m) for 200 spirit damage, executing them if they're below 18% health. After an execute you can recast it for 16s. Its passive builds your rage meter; at full rage you deal +8% damage.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +6m range; +2 m/s move speed at full rage |
| 2 | +16% full-rage damage bonus; -25s cooldown |
| 3 | +10% execute threshold |
