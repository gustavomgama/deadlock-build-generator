# Dynamo

**Tags:** Teamplay · Initiator · Clutch  
**Type:** mystic

## Abilities

### Kinetic Pulse

**Base Damage:** 115 · **Cooldown:** 26s

Release an energy pulse along the ground that deals spirit damage and knocks enemies up.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +1 charge |
| 2 | On hit: -15% bullet resist and -24% move speed for 4s |
| 3 | +135 damage and +20m cast range |

### Quantum Entanglement

**Cooldown:** 20s

Become briefly untargetable as you teleport to a target location (10m range).

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +6m cast range |
| 2 | -6s cooldown |
| 3 | Halves the duration of non-ultimate debuffs on you and restores 1 charge |

### Rejuvenating Aurora

**Cooldown:** 48s

Channel for 5s to heal yourself and nearby allies over time.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +4m move speed during the channel |
| 2 | -20s cooldown and +1s duration |
| 3 | Move and use abilities freely while channelling; heals +2.5% max health per second |

### Singularity

**Base Damage:** 75 · **Cooldown:** 250s

Channel a singularity in your hands for 2.75s, pulling nearby enemies in, stunning them and dealing spirit damage over time.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +2m radius |
| 2 | +0.75s channel duration |
| 3 | Deals +6% of the target's max health as damage |
