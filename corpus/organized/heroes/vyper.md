# Vyper

**Tags:** Gunner · Slippery · Rat-a-Tat  
**Type:** assassin

## Weapon

The weapon's recoil pattern fires bullets in the shape of an octagon with a pointy top. The bullets start with minimal spread and become extremely inaccurate the longer the gun fires. The bullets are fired in order, relative to the crosshair: center right, top left, top right, center left, bottom right, bottom left, bottom, top. Each bullet in the pattern has a distinct pitch when fired.

## Abilities

### Screwjab Dagger

**Base Damage:** 50 · **Cooldown:** 10s

Throw a dagger for 50 spirit damage that slows by 28%. Hitting the same target again stacks (up to 3) for +25 damage and an extra 12% slow per stack. 2 charges.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +1 charge |
| 2 | -8% bullet resist on hit, plus -6% per stack |
| 3 | +2 max stacks, faster charges, and a 55% charge refund on hit |

### Lethal Venom

**Base Damage:** 20 · **Cooldown:** 28s

Inject a target (10m) with venom that goes off after 3s, dealing 20 to 140 spirit damage depending on how low their health is (max damage at 30% health). It ignores Petrifying Bola's damage block.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +31.5 maximum damage |
| 2 | -40% incoming healing and -12s cooldown |
| 3 | Your bullets also build venom (4.5% per bullet) |

### Slither

Passive: slide 15% further, slide uphill, and turn faster while sliding.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +20% slide distance |
| 2 | +2 stamina |
| 3 | Sliding grants a 180 barrier for 4s (8s cooldown) |

### Petrifying Bola

**Base Damage:** 50 · **Cooldown:** 115s

Throw an explosive bola that slows every enemy in 12m by 40% for 1.5s. A direct hit petrifies the target for 2.2s instead, blocking all damage to them until it ends, when they take 180 spirit damage.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +49.5 petrify damage |
| 2 | -20s cooldown |
| 3 | +1s petrify duration; petrifies every enemy in the area |
