# The Doorman

**Tags:** Disorienting · Map Control · Mind Games  
**Type:** mystic

## Weapon

Service With A Smile can pierce through enemies. It deals 50% less damage after the first target. Doorman can gain stacks from Split Shot by hitting multiple players with the same projectile, and it also makes pierced projectiles deal 100% damage to all targets.

## Abilities

### Call Bell

**Base Damage:** 40 · **Cooldown:** 18s

Throw a bell (up to 45m) that deals 40 spirit damage on impact, then explodes for 55 more in 5.5m. The explosion reduces weapon accuracy by 40% and slows by 28% for 4s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +1 charge |
| 2 | +30 impact and +40 explosion damage |
| 3 | +4.5m radius, +26s bell lifetime and better scaling |

### Doorway

**Cooldown:** 45s

Place two connected doors up to 65m apart for 15s. Players and projectiles that enter one door come out of the other.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +20s duration |
| 2 | Gives a barrier the first time you use the portal |
| 3 | +30m cast range, +40m door distance, and scaling with spirit power |

### Luggage Cart

**Base Damage:** 60 · **Cooldown:** 30s

Summon a luggage cart that deals 60 spirit damage and drags enemies along a 20m path for 6s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +80 cart damage |
| 2 | +25m cast range; +75 damage when the cart hits a wall |
| 3 | -15s cooldown; wall impacts stun for 1.25s |

### Hotel Guest

**Base Damage:** 75 · **Cooldown:** 140s

Channel for 1s, then send a target within 7m to the Baroness Hotel for 6.5s. They take 75 spirit damage during the stay and another 125 if they fail to check out.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | Drains 1 stamina; -20s cooldown |
| 2 | +150 damage; a failed checkout stuns for 1.5s |
| 3 | You're Unstoppable while the guest is away; 15s cooldown on failed checkouts |
