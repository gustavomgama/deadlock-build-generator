# Apollo

**Tags:** Finesse · Mobility · A Cut Above  
**Type:** assassin

## Weapon

Pride of Ixia has no damage falloff and deals no damage beyond its max range.

## Abilities

### Disengaging Sigil

**Base Damage:** 85 · **Cooldown:** 12s

Draw a sigil and leap backwards as it explodes, dealing 85 spirit damage in 6.5m and slowing enemies by 24% for 4s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +25% fire rate and +25% bullet speed for 8s |
| 2 | On hit: restore 1 stamina and reset your air jump and dash |
| 3 | Can be recast within 4s |

### Riposte

**Cooldown:** 22s

Brace for 0.8s to deflect an attack. On a successful deflect you're invulnerable for 0.3s, then dash to the attacker (up to 35m), stunning them for 0.8s and reducing their melee resist by 25% for 3s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -8s cooldown |
| 2 | An extra -25% melee resist and +0.4s stun |
| 3 | 75% bullet, spirit and melee lifesteal against the target for 13s |

### Flawless Advance

**Base Damage:** 25 · **Cooldown:** 26s

Over 8s, make up to 3 lunges in any direction with piercing stabs. Hold to time each attack. A perfectly timed release deals the most damage (65, up from a base of 25).

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | Perfect hits on heroes heal you for 35 |
| 2 | -12s cooldown and +60% bullet resist while lunging |
| 3 | +65 perfect-hit damage, better spirit scaling, and faster, longer lunges |

### Itani Lo Sahn

**Base Damage:** 70 · **Cooldown:** 145s

Charge for 1.5s, then perform a long-range slash. Enemies struck are put in slow motion for 1.8s, unable to act or heal, then take 200 spirit damage, with +60% bonus damage against targets below half health. You're invulnerable during it.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +8m dash range |
| 2 | -35s cooldown |
| 3 | +50% bonus damage |
