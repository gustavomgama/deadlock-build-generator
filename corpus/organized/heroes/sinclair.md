# Sinclair

**Tags:** Trickster · Copycat · Versatile  
**Type:** mystic

## Weapon

## Abilities

### Vexing Bolt

**Base Damage:** 120 · **Cooldown:** 24s

Fire a bolt at very long range (500m) that deals 60 spirit damage in 3.25m, rising to 120 after travelling for 2s, and reduces fire rate by 25% for 5s. Your Spectral Assistant fires one too, for 50% damage.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -13s cooldown |
| 2 | An extra -25% fire rate for 5s |
| 3 | +126 maximum damage and +50% assistant damage |

### Spectral Assistant

**Base Damage:** 15 · **Cooldown:** 40s

Summon an assistant (15m) for 6s that attacks whenever you fire and reloads your gun on cast. You can swap places with it twice.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -10s cooldown |
| 2 | +7s duration and +5m range |
| 3 | +60% fire rate and +12.6 assistant damage |

### Rabbit Hex

**Cooldown:** 26s

Turn enemies in a 6m area (24m range) into rabbits for 2s. Rabbits can't act, take 15% more damage, and move faster.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -10s cooldown |
| 2 | +1s duration |
| 3 | +7% damage amp and +2m radius |

### Audience Participation

**Cooldown:** 85s

Target an enemy (20m) to copy their ultimate for 9s. The copied ultimate's cooldown is 60% of the original.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | The copied ultimate gets its tier 1 upgrade |
| 2 | The copied ultimate gets its tier 2 upgrade |
| 3 | The copied ultimate gets its tier 3 upgrade |
