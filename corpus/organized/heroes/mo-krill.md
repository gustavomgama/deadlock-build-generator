# Mo & Krill

**Tags:** Tag-Team · Initiator · Burrower  
**Type:** brawler

## Weapon

The weapon's spread pattern fires 4 bullets in the shape of a triangle, with one bullet in the center.

## Abilities

### Scorn

**Base Damage:** 50 · **Cooldown:** 13s

Deal 50 spirit damage to enemies within 9m and heal for the damage dealt, healing much more from heroes than from troopers.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +35 damage |
| 2 | -5s cooldown and +1m radius |
| 3 | Applies a stacking +15% damage taken debuff for 16s |

### Burrow

**Base Damage:** 75 · **Cooldown:** 40s

Burrow underground for 5s with +5 m/s move speed, 60% bullet resist and 30% spirit resist. When you come up, you spin for 1.5s, dealing spirit damage, slowing and knocking enemies up.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +50 spin DPS |
| 2 | +4s burrow time and +2m radius |
| 3 | -20s cooldown and +4 m/s move speed |

### Sand Blast

**Base Damage:** 40 · **Cooldown:** 40s

Spray sand in a 25m cone, dealing 40 spirit damage and disarming enemies for 2.5s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +50 damage and +5m range |
| 2 | Slows movement and dash distance |
| 3 | +1.5s disarm and -25s cooldown |

### Combo

**Base Damage:** 40 · **Cooldown:** 150s

Channel for 2.4s on a target within 4m, holding them in place and stunning them while dealing spirit damage. If they die during it or within 3s after, you permanently gain max health (40 + 2 per boon). You can use items during it.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +100% lifesteal during Combo |
| 2 | -30s cooldown and +50% bullet resist |
| 3 | +0.7s duration, +40 DPS and better spirit scaling |
