### Splatter

**Base Damage:** 55 · **Cooldown:** 26s

Throw goo that bounces twice, dealing 55 spirit damage in 5m and leaving a puddle that slows enemies by 28% for 10s. Allies slide faster on the puddles.

### The Cube

**Cooldown:** 42s

Encase a target (20m) in invulnerable goo for 3s, healing them (40 regen). They can't act, but can jump or mantle out early.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|

**Base Damage:** 20 · **Cooldown:** 24s

Summon a fist (40m range, 4m radius) that deals melee damage, knocks enemies up and slows them by 24% for 3s. Enemies can parry it.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +1 charge and +20 damage |

**Base Damage:** 110 · **Cooldown:** 150s

Turn into a big bouncing ball of goo for 11s with 35% bullet and spirit resist. Rolling into enemies deals 110 spirit damage and stuns for 0.5s. You can double jump.
