# Holliday

**Tags:** Crackshot · Explosive · Apprehender  
**Type:** marksman

## Abilities

### Powder Keg: Base Damage 13.3333

**Cooldown:** 28s

Throw an explosive barrel (2 charges) that deals 40 spirit damage on impact plus a burn for 3s in 6m. Shooting it, meleeing it or blowing up another barrel next to it detonates it early.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -10s cooldown |
| 2 | +1 charge |
| 3 | +40 impact damage, a stronger burn, faster charges and better scaling |

### Bounce Pad

**Base Damage:** 60 · **Cooldown:** 41s

Drop a pad (lasts 22s) that launches any hero who steps on it, with full air control. You explode on landing for 60 spirit damage in 9m (once per person).

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -10s cooldown |
| 2 | +4 m/s move speed for 4s on landing |
| 3 | Your landing stomp stuns for 0.7s |

### Crackshot

**Base Damage:** 55 · **Cooldown:** 20s

Your next headshot deals 55 bonus weapon damage and slows by 40% for 2s. It ignores damage falloff and doesn't work on objectives. Hitting NPCs halves the cooldown.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -20% slow strength |
| 2 | +49.5 damage; -6% bullet resist for 5s |
| 3 | Hero headshots take 6s off the cooldown; NPC hits take 3s |

### Spirit Lasso

**Base Damage:** 80 · **Cooldown:** 130s

Throw a lasso (20m) that deals 80 spirit damage and drags and stuns the target for 2.25s. Using Bounce Pad while lassoing extends it by 1.25s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +80 damage |
| 2 | +0.75s duration |
| 3 | -40s cooldown |
