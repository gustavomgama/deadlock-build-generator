# Paige

**Tags:** Helpful · Protector · Booksmart  
**Type:** mystic

## Weapon

Chapter's End's projectiles can pierce through enemies. Items that proc on bullet hit such as Mystic Shot only proc on the first enemy hit. Chapter's End does not deal critical damage on headshots, but it does proc headshot items such as Headshot Booster. Paige can gain stacks from Split Shot by hitting multiple players with the same projectile.

## Abilities

### Bookwyrm

**Base Damage:** 60 · **Cooldown:** 33s

Conjure a dragon that travels 20m, dealing 60 spirit damage and leaving a burning trail (30 DPS) for 3s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -12s cooldown |
| 2 | +1 charge, +2s trail duration and +1m radius |
| 3 | +12m travel range, +100 damage and +30 trail DPS |

### Plot Armor

**Cooldown:** 28s

Give an ally within 35m a 125 barrier for 5s, plus +25% weapon damage while the barrier holds.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +14% fire rate while the barrier holds |
| 2 | +100 barrier and +2s duration |
| 3 | Shields 2 more allies; better barrier scaling |

### Captivating Read

**Base Damage:** 90 · **Cooldown:** 30s

Target an area (30m range, 7.5m radius) that slows enemies by 36%, then explodes after 1.25s for 90 spirit damage and a 1s immobilise.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -14s cooldown |
| 2 | +1s immobilise |
| 3 | +2m radius; hit enemies get -18% spirit resist for 6s |

### Rallying Charge

**Base Damage:** 125 · **Cooldown:** 210s

Channel for 0.7s, then release a line of knights that charges across the entire map. Allies hit are healed for 125 and get +5 m/s move speed for 9s. Enemies take 125 damage and are stunned for 1s. The effect grows by up to +100% over 250m of travel.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +150 healing |
| 2 | 4 more steeds wide; -45s cooldown |
| 3 | +160 damage, +0.5s stun and +70% maximum amp |
