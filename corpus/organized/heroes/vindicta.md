# Vindicta

**Tags:** Sniper · Soaring · One Shot Kill  
**Type:** marksman

## Abilities

### Stake

**Base Damage:** 40 · **Cooldown:** 40s

Throw a stake that tethers enemies near where it lands, stopping them moving away. Deals 40 spirit damage and slows by 40% for 1.75s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +45 damage |
| 2 | -22s cooldown |
| 3 | +0.75s duration and +2m capture radius |

### Flight

**Cooldown:** 42s

Leap into the air and fly for 13s. Your bullets deal bonus spirit damage and your items get 50% more range.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +50% base ammo while flying |
| 2 | +10s duration |
| 3 | Hero kills refresh the duration; +10 spirit damage |

### Crow Familiar

**Base Damage:** 40 · **Cooldown:** 32s

Send a crow that deals 40 spirit damage, reduces the target's resistances by 6%, and makes them bleed (2.2% of their health per second) for 5s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | Target receives 35% less healing |
| 2 | +0.5% bleed damage and -16s cooldown |
| 3 | +2s duration and -8% bullet and spirit resist |

### Assassinate

**Base Damage:** 90 · **Cooldown:** 55s

A scoped, instant-hit sniper shot. Deals 90 damage, up to 180 when fully charged (1s), with bonus damage against targets below 50% health. Kills with it give a stacking +6% weapon damage. 2 charges.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -15s cooldown |
| 2 | +80 maximum bonus damage |
| 3 | +4% weapon damage per kill |
