# Drifter

**Tags:** Stalker · Bloodthirsty · Cruel  
**Type:** assassin

## Weapon

Crimson Grasp deals the same amount of bullet damage to a target regardless of how many pellets hit per shot. It deals  % weapon damage at max range (instead of the standard -90%), and deals no damage beyond its max range. The weapon's spread pattern fires 3 bullets in diagonal lines that alternate with every shot as Drifter fires from his left and right hands. The first volley fires bullets in a line from the top left to the bottom right, while the second volley goes from the top right to the bottom left. Afterwards, the pattern repeats. The pattern resets when not firing, so the first shot will always be the same shape. The spread of both shots during continuous fire forms an X shape. The additional bullets are always a fixed distance from the center, causing the weapon to cover a wide area at short ranges and a narrow area at long ranges. The additional bullets cannot [headshot](headshot.md) enemies.

**Cooldown:** 16s

| 2 | -8s cooldown |

### Stalker's Mark

**Cooldown:** 26s

Throw a mark that makes the target bleed (2% of their health per second) for 5s. Reactivate to teleport behind the marked target.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | Marked target has -8% bullet resist |
| 2 | -10s cooldown and +3s duration |
| 3 | +1.5% bleed and -40% incoming healing |

### Bloodscent

Passive: enemy heroes alone within 20m of no allies are isolated. Within 80m you can see their trail and hear their heartbeat, and you deal 15% more damage to them. Killing an isolated hero permanently gives you +3% weapon damage.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +3 m/s move speed near isolated enemies |
| 2 | On kill: heal 24% of missing health and restore 2 stamina |
| 3 | +11% more damage against isolated enemies |

### Eternal Night

**Cooldown:** 145s

Plunge up to 2 enemies (100m range) into darkness for 6.5s. Their vision shrinks to 15m, they're briefly revealed to you, and they count as isolated. You get bonus sprint speed.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +10 m/s sprint speed |
| 2 | -40s cooldown |
| 3 | +2.5s duration and +1 target |
