# Warden

**Tags:** Initiator · Fearless · One Man Army  
**Type:** brawler

## Abilities

### Alchemical Flask

**Base Damage:** 60 · **Cooldown:** 12s

Throw a flask that damages enemies and reduces their weapon damage and move speed.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | Enemies hit lose 1 stamina |
| 2 | +35 damage and -25% weapon damage on hit |
| 3 | -7s cooldown and +2m radius; applies -30% fire rate |

### Willpower

**Cooldown:** 40s

Gain a barrier and bonus move speed.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +20% move speed bonus |
| 2 | -24s cooldown and +2s duration |
| 3 | +30% debuff resist and better barrier scaling |

### Binding Word

**Base Damage:** 110 · **Cooldown:** 34s

Curse an enemy hero. If they don't move far enough from where they stood before the timer runs out, they're damaged and immobilised.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +20% bullet damage against trapped heroes for 5s |
| 2 | +0.75s duration |
| 3 | -14s cooldown; silences the target until they escape or are immobilised |

### Last Stand

**Cooldown:** 180s

Charge for 2s, then release pulses that damage nearby enemies and heal you based on the damage dealt. While channelling, you have greatly increased bullet and spirit resist.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +4m radius |
| 2 | +40.5 DPS and -30s cooldown |
| 3 | +4s duration; Unstoppable and +30% resists while channelling |
