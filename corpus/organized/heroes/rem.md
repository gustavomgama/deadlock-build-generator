# Rem

**Tags:** Helpful · Tiny · zZzzZ  
**Type:** mystic

## Abilities

### Pillow Toss

**Base Damage:** 75 · **Cooldown:** 25s

Throw a pillow (40m) that deals 75 spirit damage in 5m, knocking enemies back and slowing them by 36% for 3s. Hitting with it cuts your other cooldowns by 5s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -7s cooldown |
| 2 | +2m radius; -35% fire rate on hit |
| 3 | +100 damage and +1 charge |

### Tag Along

**Cooldown:** 40s

Fly to an ally (23m) and nap on them for up to 5.5s, healing them for 15% of missing health plus regeneration. You're untargetable while napping, and you can hop between allies, healing each once.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -8s cooldown |
| 2 | Your items get 35% more range, duration and barrier strength |
| 3 | +15% spirit power, plus bonus spirit power while and after napping |

### Lil Helpers

**Base Damage:** 20

Send helpers (45m) to follow a hero, giving them 12% spirit resist and +3 m/s move speed, or to follow a trooper, making it tougher and stronger for 50s. Helpers can also open crates, hit Sacrifices and buy from shops for you.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +1 helper and +1.5 m/s move speed |
| 2 | +1 helper and +15% trooper damage and resist |
| 3 | +1 helper and +15% spirit resist |

### Naptime

**Base Damage:** 120 · **Cooldown:** 200s

Channel for 1.9s, then put enemies in a 19m radius (24m range) to sleep for 4s. Damage wakes them (after a damage threshold), dealing 120 spirit damage. You take 30% less damage while channelling.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | Waking removes 1 stamina, and sleepers don't regenerate stamina |
| 2 | +3m radius and +0.75s sleep |
| 3 | -55s cooldown; much less damage taken and Unstoppable while channelling |
