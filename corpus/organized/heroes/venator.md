# Venator

**Tags:** Devout · Arms Expert · Tactical  
**Type:** marksman

## Abilities

### Consecrating Grenade

**Base Damage:** 35 · **Cooldown:** 25s

Throw a bouncing grenade that deals 35 weapon damage in 4.5m and sets enemies alight. Burning enemies take 10 pure damage per second and receive 30% less healing for 3.5s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -5s cooldown |
| 2 | +1.5m radius and +1s burn |
| 3 | An extra -20% healing; adds charges |

### Gutshot

**Base Damage:** 60 · **Cooldown:** 23s

Fire a shotgun blast for 60 weapon damage that pushes enemies back. Enemies near a wall are stunned for 0.6s and take 30 bonus damage.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +25 damage |
| 2 | -10s cooldown and +0.4s stun |
| 3 | After a wall stun, your next heavy melee is blessed |

### Hex-Lined Snap Trap

**Base Damage:** 80 · **Cooldown:** 28s

Kick out a trap (lasts 30s, 2 charges) that snaps on enemies for 80 spirit damage in 2m, immobilising them for 1.25s and revealing them for 6s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -11s cooldown |
| 2 | +1s immobilise |
| 3 | +1 charge; +30% damage against revealed enemies |

### Ira Domini

**Base Damage:** 120 · **Cooldown:** 160s

Draw a crossbow for 15s with 3 stakes that deal 120 weapon damage each. The final stake is blessed, dealing +115 pure damage and executing targets below 8% health.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +1.2 m/s move speed while active |
| 2 | -15s cooldown and +65 bonus damage |
| 3 | All stakes are blessed |
