# McGinnis

**Tags:** Inventor · Support · Disruption  
**Type:** mystic

## Abilities

### Mini Turret

Deploys an automated turret that shoots enemies for spirit damage. Turrets have 100 health and last 35 seconds.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +10 turret DPS and +10m attack range |
| 2 | +2 charges |
| 3 | +25% turret fire rate and +12s turret lifetime |

### Medicinal Specter

Summons a spirit that heals allies within 6m for 6.5 seconds.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +1.5s duration |
| 2 | -20s cooldown and +100% stamina recovery |
| 3 | +2% max HP regen, +3m heal radius and +40% spirit resist |

### Spectral Wall

**Base Damage:** 60

Raises a wall that slows enemies passing through it. Detonate it early to erupt and damage nearby enemies. Enemies can break it with melee.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | McGinnis deals +20% damage to enemies hit for 7s |
| 2 | -20s cooldown and +2s duration |
| 3 | Spawns 2 Mini Turrets by the wall for 8s; +24% slow |

### Heavy Barrage

Fires homing rockets at a target area for 8 seconds. McGinnis is slowed while firing, but can still use stamina.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | Rockets apply a 24% move and dash slow for 1s |
| 2 | -45s cooldown and +6s duration |
| 3 | +2m radius and +15 damage per rocket |
