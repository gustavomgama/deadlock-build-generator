# Mirage

**Tags:** Bodyguard · Traveller · Focused  
**Type:** assassin

## Abilities

### Fire Scarabs

**Base Damage:** 8 · **Cooldown:** 35s

Infest an enemy with scarabs for 5s, stealing 8 health per second and reducing their damage by 20%. 2 charges.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +7 DPS |
| 2 | +1 charge and +2s duration |
| 3 | An extra -15% damage penalty and better spirit scaling |

### Dust Devil

**Base Damage:** 65 · **Cooldown:** 36s

Become a whirlwind that travels forward (20m), dealing 65 spirit damage, briefly lifting enemies and slowing them by 24% for 3s. You get 30% bullet evasion for 4s afterwards.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +60 damage |
| 2 | -12s cooldown and +30% evasion |
| 3 | Recast within 6s; better spirit scaling |

### Djinn's Mark

**Base Damage:** 11

Passive: your shots mark the target (once every 2.75s per target, up to 4 marks). When marks expire or are consumed they deal 11 spirit damage, doubling with each extra mark. Marked targets are revealed for 6s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | Procs slow by 48% for 0.8s |
| 2 | +20 base damage and +3s duration |
| 3 | -0.75s cooldown, +1 max mark, and a 0.5s stun at max marks |

### Traveler

**Cooldown:** 140s

Pick a location on the minimap, wait 2s, then teleport there. Taking damage interrupts it.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +3 m/s move speed and +20% fire rate for 12s after arriving |
| 2 | 400 barrier (scales with spirit power) |
| 3 | -90s cooldown |
