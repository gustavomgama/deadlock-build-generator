# Seven

**Tags:** High Voltage · Merciless · Area Denial  
**Type:** mystic

## Abilities

### Lightning Ball

**Base Damage:** 75 · **Cooldown:** 26s

Fires a slow-moving ball of lightning that damages everything within 4.25m (75 DPS) for 5s and slows on hit. Uses charges.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +1 charge |
| 2 | Applies a 28% move slow; +1s lifetime |
| 3 | +58.5 DPS and +1.75m radius |

### Static Charge

**Base Damage:** 35 · **Cooldown:** 42s

Charges a target enemy (15m range). After 3.5s it discharges, damaging and stunning enemies within 5m for 0.9s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -20s cooldown |
| 2 | +7m radius and +5m cast range |
| 3 | +0.9s stun and +160 damage |

### Power Surge

**Base Damage:** 10 · **Cooldown:** 50s

Empowers your gun for 10s. Bullets fire shocks (10 damage) that bounce to up to 4 nearby enemies within 10m.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -18s cooldown |
| 2 | +3m move speed and +8 damage |
| 3 | +10s duration; shocks apply -15% spirit resist |

### Storm Cloud

**Base Damage:** 95 · **Cooldown:** 205s

Rise into the air and channel a storm for 7s that grows from 10m to 30m, dealing 95 DPS to everyone inside. Press fire for 75-damage lightning strikes.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +55% bullet resist while channelling |
| 2 | +7s channel, +5m starting radius and +10m final radius |
| 3 | +65 DPS and +4m flight speed |
