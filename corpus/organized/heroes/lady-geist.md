# Lady Geist

**Tags:** Lifesteal · Self Damage · Fatale  
**Type:** mystic

## Abilities

### Essence Bomb

**Base Damage:** 90 · **Cooldown:** 14s

Sacrifice 30% of your current health to throw a bomb that deals 90 spirit damage in a 7m radius after a short arming time.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -5s cooldown |
| 2 | +2m radius and +50 damage |
| 3 | Leaves a toxic mess that deals 30% of the bomb's damage per second for 6s |

### Life Drain

**Cooldown:** 34s

Tether to an enemy (18m cast range, breaks at 28m) to drain 24 DPS for 2.5s, healing you for 100% of the damage dealt.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +13 DPS |
| 2 | +2.5s duration |
| 3 | +2 charges and better spirit scaling |

### Malice

**Base Damage:** 23 · **Cooldown:** 6s

Spend a little health to launch blood shards. Each hit adds a stack (up to 5) that amplifies damage taken by 7% and slows by 12%.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -3s cooldown |
| 2 | +25.2 damage and +4 blood shards |
| 3 | +8% damage amplification per stack |

### Soul Exchange

**Cooldown:** 220s

Swap health percentages with a nearby enemy (5.5m range). Neither of you can go below 30% health. The damage dealt is pure damage, which ignores resistances.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -50s cooldown |
| 2 | Silences enemies within 25m for 3s |
| 3 | +60 spirit power, +40% fire rate and +50% spirit resist for 8s |
