# Solomon

**Tags:** Strategist · Focused · Checkmate  
**Type:** mystic

## Weapon

Solomon cannot zoom in by default, as his right-click is used to move his ability pieces. Projectiles explode in a ~1m radius for full damage, including headshot damage. The explosions can only hit enemies once per shot, even when additional explosions are created with Split Shot, or Armor Piercer. Ricochet can damage enemies twice if they are first hit by an explosion and then directly hit by a ricocheting projectile. This also applies [Build-Up](Build-Up.md) effects twice as quickly. Mystic Shot will only affect one target per shot.

## Abilities

### Setup Bishop

**Base Damage:** 25 · **Cooldown:** 12s

Place a bishop up to 20m away. It hits for 70 spirit damage on landing and stays on the board until you recall it. Alt-fire slides it up to 12m (every 3s), damaging and slowing enemies it passes through by 30% for 3s. After a short delay, the squares on its diagonals deal 25 spirit damage to enemies who step on them. Press it again to recall, which starts the 12s cooldown.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +5m move distance and +15% slow |
| 2 | +1 bishop, recast within 8s |
| 3 | +1 square range, and squares stack -2.5% spirit resist (up to 10 times) |

### Kingside Knight

**Cooldown:** 25s

Turn an ally up to 28m away into a knight for 7s, giving them an 80 barrier and 1 extra stamina, then jump them up to 13m. They can't be hurt during the move. Cast it on yourself and you become the king instead, with double the barrier and a shorter 7m move, but no stamina.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -8s cooldown |
| 2 | +80 barrier and +1 stamina |
| 3 | Hits two allies in range; on yourself, the move cleanses debuffs (not stuns or ultimates) |

### Rook Battery

**Base Damage:** 50 · **Cooldown:** 33s

Fire a pair of rooks that deal 50 spirit damage and root enemies in place for 1s. They bounce off one wall. Press it again while they travel to split them sideways and cover more ground.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | Hit enemies lose 25% fire rate for 5s |
| 2 | Hits also deal 5% of the target's current health |
| 3 | +1 wall bounce, -12s cooldown and +0.5s root |

### Develop Queen

**Base Damage:** 120 · **Cooldown:** 210s

Drop the queen up to 30m away for 120 spirit damage around her. After a short delay, lines of squares shoot out from her in every direction, hitting again and stunning for 1.25s. You get two more moves (up to 16m each, every 5s), and enemies she passes through are slowed by 15%.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -40s cooldown |
| 2 | +15% slow, and checkmate: executes heroes under 200 health |
| 3 | +2 moves and +1 square range |
