# Abrams

**Tags:** Tank · Brawler · Bull-Headed  
**Type:** brawler

## Weapon

Case Closed has a 0.7s delay to start [reloading](Reload Time.md) after shooting a shell. It takes 0.35s to start reloading and 0.35s to reload each shell. Reloading all shells takes 3.5s. The weapon's spread pattern fires 9 bullets in the shape of a square, with one bullet at the center. The other bullets are inaccurate and have a wide spread.

## Abilities

### Siphon Life

**Base Damage:** 22 · **Cooldown:** 42s

Drains health from every enemy within 8m for 4 seconds, dealing spirit damage over time and healing Abrams for part of the damage dealt.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -20s cooldown |
| 2 | +2s duration |
| 3 | +2m radius and +18 DPS, with better spirit scaling |

### Shoulder Charge

**Base Damage:** 30 · **Cooldown:** 33s

Charges forward (about 17.5m), dragging enemies along. Slamming them into a wall stuns them. Abrams speeds up when he collides with heroes.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | Applies a 32% slow for 3s |
| 2 | Wall hits stun for +0.8s longer |
| 3 | -18s cooldown; colliding with a hero gives +1.5 weapon damage for 6s |

### Infernal Resilience

Passive. Abrams regenerates 13% of the damage he takes over the next 20 seconds.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +200 max health |
| 2 | +18% melee lifesteal |
| 3 | +20% debuff resist and +9% damage regenerated |

### Seismic Impact

**Base Damage:** 100 · **Cooldown:** 215s

Leaps high into the air and crashes down, dealing spirit damage and stunning enemies within 9m for 1.6 seconds.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -30s cooldown |
| 2 | +0.8s stun duration |
| 3 | Unstoppable for 5s on cast; +6m impact radius |
