# Victor

**Tags:** You · Can't · Kill Me  
**Type:** brawler

## Weapon

Victor's weapon takes 1.67s to spin up and reach max fire rate and bullet spread.

## Abilities

### Pain Battery

**Base Damage:** 100 · **Cooldown:** 2s

Passively charges as you take damage (one charge per 40% of max health). Activate to fire shocking bolts that deal 100 spirit damage.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | Bolts slow heroes by 32% for 2s |
| 2 | +50 damage |
| 3 | Heal 15% of missing health on hero hit; better spirit scaling |

### Jumpstart

**Base Damage:** 15 · **Cooldown:** 30s

Shock yourself for 15% of your current health, then gain a burst of health regen (100) and +3 m/s move speed that fade over 4.5s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +3 m/s move speed |
| 2 | +70 health regen and -8s cooldown |
| 3 | +1 charge, +50% debuff resist and better spirit scaling |

### Aura of Suffering

**Cooldown:** 2s

Channel an aura (8m, 8s) that deals growing spirit damage to nearby enemies (13 up to 58 DPS). You also take 70% of it yourself. Deals 50% damage to objectives.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | Enemies are slowed by 20% (move and dash) |
| 2 | +6 minimum and +34 maximum DPS |
| 3 | +1m radius; enemies take +15% damage |

### Shocking Reanimation

**Base Damage:** 200 · **Cooldown:** 240s

Passive: when you take lethal damage, you release a slowing wave instead of dying, then after 3s reanimate at 50% health, dealing 200 spirit damage and stunning enemies within 18m for 1.5s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | While it's on cooldown, +15% fire rate and +6 spirit damage per bullet |
| 2 | +50% revive health |
| 3 | +175 damage, +1.5s stun and -95s cooldown |
