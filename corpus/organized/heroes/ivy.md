# Ivy

**Tags:** Team-up · Disruptor · Rock Solid  
**Type:** marksman

## Abilities

### Entangling Thorns

**Base Damage:** 40 · **Cooldown:** 32s

Summon a patch of thorns (6m, 4s) that deals 40 spirit DPS and slows by 28%.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +1 charge |
| 2 | +2m radius and better DPS scaling |
| 3 | Enemies slowed for 2s are immobilised for 1.6s |

### Kudzu Connection

**Cooldown:** 37s

Tether to an ally (16m, needs line of sight) for 12s. You both get +10% fire rate and +15% bullet lifesteal, and 35% of healing is shared. With no connection, you get +50% move speed.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +2 m/s move speed |
| 2 | +8% fire rate and +8% bullet lifesteal |
| 3 | Always active |

### Stone Form

**Base Damage:** 75 · **Cooldown:** 40s

Turn to stone for 3s, stunning enemies within 5.75m for 0.75s, dealing 75 damage and healing yourself for 6% of max health.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +6% max health heal |
| 2 | -25s cooldown |
| 3 | +0.75s stun and better damage scaling |

### Air Drop

**Base Damage:** 115 · **Cooldown:** 100s

Grab an ally (22m) and fly with them for up to 21s, then drop them for a 115-damage explosion in 20m and a +20% damage buff for 8s. Cast on yourself to drop a bomb instead. Cooldown is 70s when used on an ally.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | 300 barrier for both of you |
| 2 | Explosion slows by 32% for 3s |
| 3 | Explosion silences for 3s; better damage and barrier scaling |
