# Mina

**Tags:** Harasser · Nimble · Vexing  
**Type:** marksman

## Abilities

### Rake

**Base Damage:** 60 · **Cooldown:** 16s

Slash with your umbrella (10m) for 60 spirit damage plus 6% of the target's missing health. Executes non-heroes below 60 health and heals you for each target killed. Its passive lets you hold zoom to float briefly (up to 4s).

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +60 damage |
| 2 | +30 healing per kill and -8s cooldown |
| 3 | +7% missing health damage and better healing scaling |

### Sanguine Retreat

**Cooldown:** 32s

Burst into bats, becoming untargetable, and fly to a location (9m). You can recast it within 4s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | On cast: +25% fire rate for 8s and 8 extra bullets |
| 2 | -10s cooldown |
| 3 | +1 recast and +4m range |

### Love Bites

**Base Damage:** 45

Passive: your bullets deal 4 bonus spirit damage, and your ability damage builds up to a vicious bite that deals 45 bonus spirit damage. Each target can only be bitten once every 10s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | Bites slow by 24% for 3s |
| 2 | +3 spirit damage per bullet and +45 bite damage |
| 3 | -5s per-target cooldown; bites give +25% fire rate for 5s |

### Nox Nostra: Base Damage 4.6

**Cooldown:** 150s

Release a seeking bat swarm (40m) that deals spirit damage and silences for 1.25s. Its passive permanently adds 2 bats for every Love Bites proc on a hero (up to 75).

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +1.9 damage per bat |
| 2 | -45s cooldown |
| 3 | Bats deal +0.5% of current health as damage |
