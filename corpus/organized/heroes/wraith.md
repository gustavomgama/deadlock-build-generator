# Wraith

**Tags:** Duelist · Isolator · Telekinetic  
**Type:** marksman

## Abilities

### Card Trick

**Base Damage:** 45

Dealing weapon damage summons cards of a random suit. Throw them for damage plus an effect that depends on the suit, for example Hearts heal you and Diamonds reduce the target's resist. Holds 2 charges.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +2 charges |
| 2 | +40 damage and better spirit scaling |
| 3 | All suits improved and a higher chance of a Joker |

### Project Mind

**Cooldown:** 46s

Teleport to a target location within 25m.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +15m cast range |
| 2 | 300 barrier for 5s after teleporting (scales with spirit power) |
| 3 | -32s cooldown |

### Full Auto

**Cooldown:** 45s

For 5s, gain +20% fire rate and deal bonus spirit damage on every bullet.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -20s cooldown |
| 2 | +10% fire rate and +3s duration |
| 3 | Unlimited ammo during Full Auto and better spirit damage scaling |

### Telekinesis

**Base Damage:** 100 · **Cooldown:** 150s

Channel for 1.25s to lift an enemy hero into the air, then slam them towards a target location (13m throw range).

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +100 damage |
| 2 | -45s cooldown |
| 3 | +1.5s debuff duration and +6m range |
