# Deadman Danny

**Tags:** Reckless · High-flying · Explosive  
**Type:** marksman

## Weapon

The Chimera Peace Bringer is a weapon with two firing modes: uncharged and charged. Holding the firing button charges a shot. Releasing before it reaches maximum charge fires an uncharged shot, which uses Danny's visible bullet damage stat. If the button is held until maximum charge, the weapon immediately fires a charged shot. Charged shots consume two ammo instead of one, deal splash damage on impact, and travel at 66 m/s. The explosion deals 48 base [Weapon Damage](Weapon Damage.md) in a 2.5 m radius and is affected by [Ability Range](Ability Range.md). It has radial damage falloff, dealing only  % damage at its edge. Charged shots will contribute 3x the buildup on items with buildup based de-buffs (Slowing Bullets, Toxic Bullets, etc.). Charged shots can also damage Danny himself, knocking him back similarly to a [Rocket Jump](wikipedia_Rocket jumping.md). The self-damage radius for charged shots is 0.25 m larger than the splash radius and deals 5% of Danny's maximum health as [spirit damage](Spirit Power.md#Spirit-Damage). If an enemy hero is also hit by the shot, Danny takes no self-damage but is still knocked back. Neither firing mode can [headshot](headshot.md), so Danny cannot purchase headshot items. Danny also cannot purchase Ricochet. If it is forcibly equipped via commands, its ricochet will always be an uncharged shot.

## Abilities

### Strix Salvo

**Base Damage:** 27 · **Cooldown:** 25s

Hold and drag to aim a line of spirit missiles up to 35m away. They lodge in the ground and explode when an enemy comes near (or after 6s) for 27 spirit damage, marking whoever they hit for 10s. A charged shot into a marked enemy uses up the mark for 45 bonus spirit damage and a 40% slow that fades over 1.5s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -12s cooldown |
| 2 | +30 damage and +45 marked damage |
| 3 | +2 missiles, longer range and better spirit scaling |

### Chimera Mk1

**Base Damage:** 70 · **Cooldown:** 21s

Hold jump to fly while the jetpack heats up. When it overheats, Danny dives and explodes where he lands for 70 spirit and 70 weapon damage, launching nearby enemies. He takes 10% of his max health as self-damage, and if the explosion misses every hero he stuns himself for 1.3s. Press the ability again to dive early. The jetpack can't be used again until it has fully cooled.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | Heat vents faster while he is on the ground |
| 2 | Hitting a hero heals him and removes the self-damage |
| 3 | Hitting a hero takes 40% off the cooldown and adds +120 spirit damage |

### Jettison Fuel

**Base Damage:** 30 · **Cooldown:** 22s

Spray fuel up to 22m for 2s, dealing 30 weapon damage per second and shredding 15% bullet resist for 7s. The spray builds to a backblast that deals 35 spirit damage and knocks Danny backwards. Damage dealt with it vents heat from the jetpack.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -8s cooldown |
| 2 | +60 backblast damage; hitting with the backblast gives +18% fire rate for 7s |
| 3 | -10% more bullet resist shred; the backblast applies a Strix Salvo mark |

### Close The Deal

**Base Damage:** 70 · **Cooldown:** 135s

Grab an enemy within 10m, fly high and crash back down with them. Both of you take spirit damage while falling (3 per metre), then 70 spirit damage and a 0.5s stun on impact. It deals 75% more damage to enemies under half health. Danny takes 25% self-damage, but his innate means his own damage can never kill him.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +90 damage |
| 2 | -35s cooldown |
| 3 | +0.5s stun and +8 damage per metre |
