# Paradox

**Tags:** Calculated · Disruptor · Tactician  
**Type:** marksman

## Abilities

### Pulse Grenade

**Base Damage:** 35 · **Cooldown:** 32s

Throw a grenade that pulses, dealing spirit damage, slowing time for enemies, and building stacking damage amplification.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -12s cooldown |
| 2 | +20 pulse damage and better spirit scaling |
| 3 | +4% damage amp and +1.6s duration |

### Time Wall

**Cooldown:** 25s

Create a time-warping wall. Enemy bullets and projectiles that touch it are stopped in time, enemies who touch it are briefly slowed, and friendly bullets passing through get faster and stronger.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +3.5s duration, +3m width and +1m height |
| 2 | Silences enemies for 2.3s; +35% weapon damage for friendly bullets |
| 3 | +2 charges |

### Kinetic Carbine

**Base Damage:** 5 · **Cooldown:** 28s

Charge up a shot of time energy that deals spirit damage and applies a time-stop to enemies hit. Its damage grows with your weapon damage, and you move faster once it's fully charged.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +0.4s maximum time-stop |
| 2 | -12s cooldown and better scaling |
| 3 | Hold the charge 2s longer; +55% damage scaling |

### Paradoxical Swap

**Base Damage:** 150 · **Cooldown:** 110s

Fire a projectile that swaps your position with the enemy it hits.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | 200 barrier for 8s (scales with spirit power) |
| 2 | -30s cooldown and +13m cast range |
| 3 | Swaps every enemy within 7m of the target too; +10% max health damage |
