# Rat King

**Tags:** Scrappy · Regal · Tenacious  
**Type:** brawler

## Weapon

One of the two pellets is always perfectly accurate to the aim point, while the other is not.

## Abilities

### Scrap Grenade

**Base Damage:** 70 · **Cooldown:** 26s

Throw a bouncing grenade that detonates 3 times, each blast dealing 70 spirit damage in an 8m radius and slowing by 20% for 4s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -8s cooldown |
| 2 | +70 damage |
| 3 | Adds a bigger final detonation |

### Rat Swarm

**Cooldown:** 24s

Send a swarm of rats to an area up to 50m away (10m radius, 6s). Up to 5 rats latch onto each enemy, biting for 8 spirit damage per second each and stripping 1% bullet resist per bite.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +1 charge |
| 2 | Wider swarm and +4s duration |
| 3 | +8 damage and an extra -1% bullet resist per bite |

### Royal Pestments

**Cooldown:** 22s

Gain a barrier worth 15% of your max health for 6s, with a 15% chance to reflect bullets back at attackers.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +4s duration |
| 2 | -8s cooldown and +15% reflect chance |
| 3 | Caps damage taken per hit at 250; +10% barrier |

### "Rule, Ratannia!"

**Base Damage:** 200 · **Cooldown:** 160s

Charge up the banner of Ratannia (up to 10s). Allies near the banner (20m) get +3 m/s move speed, slow immunity and 15% damage reduction. Then slam it into the ground. The sewers erupt for 200 bullet damage across 20m, knocking up and slowing enemies by 40%, and rats swarm out to apply Rat Swarm. The banner lasts 12s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +2 m/s move speed in the aura |
| 2 | +5s banner duration and +8m radius |
| 3 | +150 slam damage and +10% damage reduction |
