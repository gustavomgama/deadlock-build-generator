# Silver

**Tags:** Feral · Hot Mess · Transformation  
**Type:** brawler

## Weapon

Hair of the Dog reloads single shots and the reload can be interrupted. Melees and dashes do not pause the gun's cycle time (time between each shot). The weapon's spread pattern fires 7 bullets in the shape of a hexagon with a flat top, with one bullet in the center. While Slam Fire is active, the additional bullets are fired randomly in a cone instead. Loose Muzzle deals [Weapon Damage](Weapon Damage.md) in a close range, cone-shaped attack that can hit multiple enemies. It has no ammo count, no reload, and does not deal headshot damage. The 7 meter range cannot be increased by items such as Sharpshooter. Ricochet's passive is triggered for every enemy hit by Loose Muzzle and stack Lycan Curse's [Fire Rate](Fire Rate.md) bonus for each bullet proc. Split Shot has no effect on Loose Muzzle. Express Shot cannot be triggered by Loose Muzzle. Restorative Shot and Mystic Shot are not triggered if no target is hit. Lightning Scroll is triggered by Loose Muzzle. Mercurial Magnum uses the base weapon damage of a single pellet from Hair of the Dog instead of Loose Muzzle.

## Abilities

### Slam Fire

**Base Damage:** 40 · **Cooldown:** 25s

Instantly reload and fire your next 3 shots at +300% fire rate, each dealing 2.5% of current health as bonus damage, with less accuracy. Deals double damage to NPCs.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +15% weapon damage |
| 2 | -10s cooldown |
| 3 | +7% current health damage if all shots hit the same target |

### Boot Kick

**Base Damage:** 25 · **Cooldown:** 21s

Dash forward and kick the first enemy for 45 spirit damage, marking them. Shooting a marked target deals +25 spirit damage.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -6s cooldown |
| 2 | Restore 2 stamina on hero hit |
| 3 | +80 bonus damage; kicked enemies deal 35% less damage for 5s |

### Entangling Bola

**Base Damage:** 40 · **Cooldown:** 23s

Throw a bola for 40 spirit damage that slows by 16% for 1.5s and stops the target using movement abilities and stamina. Interrupts flying.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +20% move speed |
| 2 | -8s cooldown |
| 3 | +0.75s duration; ricochets to 2 more targets |

### Lycan Curse: Base Damage 0.255

**Cooldown:** 60s

Damage you deal builds bloodlust (faster at low health). At max bloodlust you transform automatically after 3s, or you can activate it. As a werewolf (15s), you gain +125 max health, move speed and fire rate stacks, and new abilities and attacks.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +15% bullet and spirit resist |
| 2 | +4 m/s move speed and +200 bonus health |
| 3 | Hero kills refresh your abilities and the transformation's duration |
