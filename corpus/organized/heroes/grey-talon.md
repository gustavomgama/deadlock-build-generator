# Grey Talon

**Tags:** Precision · Hunter · Area Denial  
**Type:** marksman

## Abilities

### Charged Shot

**Base Damage:** 80 · **Cooldown:** 17s

Charge a piercing arrow that deals 80 spirit damage. You can hold the charge indefinitely.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +1 charge |
| 2 | +54 damage |
| 3 | Better scaling and 3s less charge time |

### Rain of Arrows

**Cooldown:** 25s

Launch into the air and glide for 4s, firing 5 arrows per shot with +3 weapon damage. Alt-cast for a lower jump.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +3 damage; arrows slow by 24% for 1.5s |
| 2 | -12s cooldown |
| 3 | +30% bullet and spirit lifesteal and +30% evasion |

### Spirit Snare

**Base Damage:** 25 · **Cooldown:** 34s

Deploy a trap (6.5m radius, lasts 22s, arms in 2s). When an enemy comes near, it curses them for 2.25s, silencing, disarming, slowing and interrupting them. Shoot it with Charged Shot to detonate it early with a bigger radius.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -20s cooldown |
| 2 | -15% bullet resist for 10s |
| 3 | +1s duration and +1.5m radius |

### Guided Owl

**Base Damage:** 230 · **Cooldown:** 125s

Launch a spirit owl you steer for up to 20s. It explodes for 230 spirit damage in 12m and stuns for 0.75s. Each hero killed within 4s of being hit permanently gives you +8 spirit power.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +85 damage |
| 2 | -40s cooldown |
| 3 | Executes enemies below 22% health (pure damage) |
