# Haze

**Tags:** Assassin · Stealthy · Lethal  
**Type:** assassin

## Abilities

### Sleep Dagger

**Base Damage:** 65 · **Cooldown:** 30s

Throws a dagger that damages the target and puts them to sleep. Damage wakes them up.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | On wake-up, the target has 10% less bullet resist for 6s |
| 2 | +1s sleep duration and applies 15 Fixation stacks |
| 3 | -17s cooldown; on wake-up, -40% move and dash speed for 3s |

### Smoke Bomb

**Cooldown:** 33s

Haze fades out of sight, becoming invisible for 8 seconds and gaining sprint speed. Attacking reveals her.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +7m invisible sprint speed |
| 2 | 2 charges |
| 3 | Dispels non-ultimate debuffs and gives +50% bullet lifesteal for 5s |

### Fixation

Passive. Each bullet hit on a target adds a stack (three for a headshot) that increases Haze's bullet damage against that target. Stacks last 6s, up to 40.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | Every 20 stacks: 40 spirit damage and a 12% slow for 2s |
| 2 | +40 max stacks and +5s duration |
| 3 | +0.11 damage per stack, scaling with weapon damage |

### Bullet Dance

**Cooldown:** 165s

For 3.5 seconds, Haze fires at all nearby enemies with perfect accuracy.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +7 weapon damage |
| 2 | +10% fire rate and +3m move speed |
| 3 | +40% evasion and -65s cooldown |
