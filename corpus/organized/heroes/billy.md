# Billy

**Tags:** Punk · Chaotic · G.O.A.T.  
**Type:** brawler

## Weapon

Due to Billy's Blasted passive, hitting a [melee attack](Melee Attack.md) reloads his ammo by either 100% (heavy melee) or % (light melee). The weapon's recoil pattern fires bullets in the shape of an octagon with a pointy top. The bullets are fired in order, relative to the crosshair: center right, top left, top right, center left, bottom right, bottom left, bottom, top. Each bullet in the pattern has a distinct pitch when fired. The first bullet fired after holding down the trigger will always be perfectly centered on the crosshair instead of following the pattern, but the bullets afterwards are slightly inaccurate. The pattern persists after letting go of the trigger or reloading and will resume the next time the gun starts firing.

## Abilities

### Bashdown

**Base Damage:** 35 · **Cooldown:** 35s

Slam your bat into the ground, pulling nearby enemies down and dealing melee damage, then send out a shockwave that deals spirit damage and knocks enemies up.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -10s cooldown |
| 2 | +1 charge and +2m radius |
| 3 | Charges recharge 3s faster; the slam deals 50% of your heavy melee damage |

### Rising Ram

**Base Damage:** 40 · **Cooldown:** 32s

Charge headfirst into an enemy and launch both of you into the air, dealing spirit damage. Hitting a target halves the cooldown.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | On impact: +25% weapon damage for 5s |
| 2 | Longer charge (0.4s) |
| 3 | -13s cooldown; on impact gain +10% max health for 16s |

### Blasted

**Cooldown:** 27s

Passive: melee hits refill ammo (100% on heavy, 35% on light) and mark enemies as Wrecked for 7s. Active (8s): your bullets deal 10% more to Wrecked enemies, and each melee hit adds max health and extends the duration.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +2.25 m/s move speed while Blasted |
| 2 | Using it adds a Bashdown charge; +7% bonus against Wrecked enemies |
| 3 | +50 bonus health per melee hit and better spirit scaling |

### Chain Gang

**Base Damage:** 120 · **Cooldown:** 175s

Chain every enemy within 12m. Chained enemies can't use movement abilities and are heavily slowed if they pull against the chain. After 2.8s, all of them are yanked to you and take 120 spirit damage. Chains break if line of sight is lost for about a second.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +40% bullet and spirit resist during the chain |
| 2 | -40s cooldown |
| 3 | +5m range; 1.3s of Unstoppable per hero pulled |
