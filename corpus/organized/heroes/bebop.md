# Bebop

**Tags:** Hook · Bomb · Punch  
**Type:** brawler

## Weapon

Bebop's weapon has a unique "Max Range" stat (not to be confused with [Falloff Range](Falloff Range.md)). His weapon deals no damage beyond its max range. The max range starts at 32m and increases by +2m with [boons](Boon.md). It also has a short wind-up time to start shooting. The gun can also be wound-up by zooming with right-click. The beam will not go through paradox time wall.

## Abilities

### Exploding Uppercut: Base Damage 0.01

**Cooldown:** 22s

An uppercut that knocks the target back. When they land, it deals 75 spirit damage in 14m and slows fire rate by 14%. It also works on allies.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -11s cooldown |
| 2 | On hero hit: +30% weapon damage and double weapon range for 9s |
| 3 | On hero hit: resets Grapple Arm's cooldown and heals 18% of missing health |

### Sticky Bomb

**Base Damage:** 85 · **Cooldown:** 18s

Attach a bomb to a target (6m range) that explodes after 3.5s for 85 spirit damage in 8m. Each hero hit permanently increases its damage by 1%, and each hero kill by 2.5%.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -8s cooldown |
| 2 | +85 damage |
| 3 | On cast: +5 m/s move speed and +25% debuff resist for 6s |

### Grapple Arm

**Cooldown:** 23s

Launch a mechanical hand (30m range) that pulls the first target hit to you, dealing melee damage and slowing them by 72% for 0.5s.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +20% weapon damage against the target for 6s |
| 2 | +30m cast range |
| 3 | -11.5s cooldown |

### Hyper Beam

**Base Damage:** 160 · **Cooldown:** 120s

Channel an enormous beam for 11s, dealing 160 spirit DPS along 70m and slowing by 20%. Deals 50% damage to objectives.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | -20s cooldown |
| 2 | +108 DPS |
| 3 | Heals you for 65% of the damage dealt to heroes (20% to non-heroes) |
