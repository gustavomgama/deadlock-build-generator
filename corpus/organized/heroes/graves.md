# Graves

**Tags:** Morbid · Area Denial · Necromancer  
**Type:** mystic

## Weapon

The Teacher attaches to the target closest to the reticle. It cannot deal headshots and Graves cannot buy headshot items. However, it deals double damage once every 13 consecutive shots (~1.3s at base [fire rate](fire rate.md)). This count resets on reload, loss of target, or when attacking a different target. The Teacher has no [damage falloff](Damage Falloff.md) and deals no damage beyond its max range. However, the falloff range is factored in when calculating Graves' [Build-Up](Build-Up.md) per bullet, most notably the build-up from Essence Theft. As an exception, the effect from Ricochet has damage falloff, calculated based on the distance from the main target to the ricocheted target. [Air Dash](Air Dash.md)ing while firing The Teacher will stop consuming ammo briefly. This includes [Down Dash](Down Dash.md)ing, which costs half as much stamina and can be done without moving out of position in exchange for not being able to [slide](slide.md). The pause on ammo consumption is not shortened by [Fire Rate](Fire Rate.md). It is also the only weapon in the game that is not interrupted and can be continuously fired while performing an air dash.

## Abilities

### Jar of Dead

**Base Damage:** 16 · **Cooldown:** 13s per charge

Passively collect death from nearby kills into a jar (up to 4 charges). Throw it to summon 4 Deadheads that hit enemies (16 spirit damage each) or follow you.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | Collecting death heals 5 health per pickup |
| 2 | Deadhead hits slow by 24% for 1s |
| 3 | +2 Deadheads and +4s Deadhead lifetime |

### Grasping Hands

**Base Damage:** 90 · **Cooldown:** 34s

Raise a 24m line of grasping hands for 5s, summoning a ghoul. Enemies passing through take 90 spirit damage and are immobilised for 1s. Alt-cast rotates the wall.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +2s duration |
| 2 | +90 damage and +10m wall length |
| 3 | +1 ghoul, -14s cooldown and +0.75s immobilise |

### Essence Theft

**Base Damage:** 25

For 4s, your weapon steals up to 25% of each target's weapon damage and 10% of their spirit resist (up to 3 targets). The steal builds up over 4s on each target. Melee hits steal too.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +5% maximum spirit resist stolen |
| 2 | +20% maximum weapon damage stolen |
| 3 | +1 target; all your summons apply Essence Theft |

### Borrowed Decree

**Base Damage:** 115 · **Cooldown:** 140s

Place a gravestone (20m range) that keeps summoning ghouls (one every 4s) for 16s. Enemies destroy it with 2 heavy melees. Your abilities also summon ghouls innately; they march towards the enemy base and explode near heroes and objectives for 115 spirit damage and a 64% slow.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +25% ghoul speed and -15s cooldown |
| 2 | +10s duration and ghouls spawn 0.3s faster |
| 3 | Ghouls deal +5% of current health as damage when they die |
