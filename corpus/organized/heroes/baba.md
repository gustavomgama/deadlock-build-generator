# Baba

**Tags:** Cackling · Beady · Hexing  
**Type:** mystic

## Weapon

Bunko-Night Special deals the same amount of bullet damage to a target regardless of how many pellets hit per shot.

## Abilities

### Threadsap

**Base Damage:** 50 · **Cooldown:** 28s

Fire a spool of thread along your crosshair (35m). It bursts along its path for 50 spirit damage and steals 10 spirit power per hit, up to 45. Baba gets 175% value from what she steals, and while she holds stolen spirit, every bullet deals 8 bonus spirit damage. The steal lasts 22s, and ends early if Baba dies.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +8 spirit damage per bullet |
| 2 | -12s cooldown and +8s steal duration |
| 3 | +1 charge, and hits pull enemies 7m |

### Granny Long Legs

**Base Damage:** 75 · **Cooldown:** 32s

Her bench sprouts legs for 10s, giving +5 m/s move speed, higher jumps, 30% debuff resist and 30% melee resist. Light melee becomes a kick that knocks enemies back 10m; heavy melee becomes a ground pound (8m) that knocks up, deals 75 spirit damage and slows by 50% for 4s. Hold crouch to charge a high jump. Chained light melees in the air carry her forward less each time.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +2.5 m/s move speed |
| 2 | -7s cooldown |
| 3 | +15% resists and +50% melee lifesteal |

### Baba's Brew

**Cooldown:** 27s

Channel for 1s and splash a 6m area up to 12m away. The brew cycles on its own between three effects. Soothing gives allies a 90 barrier for 8s, Silencing silences enemies for 3s, and Scalding burns enemies for 12 spirit damage per second over 8s and lowers their spirit resist. Alt-fire pauses the cycle so you can hold the brew you want.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +3m radius |
| 2 | -12s cooldown |
| 3 | +33% brew effectiveness |

### Feed The Birds

**Base Damage:** 450 · **Cooldown:** 150s

Channel for up to 6.5s, throwing bread at enemies in front of you (18m). Bread stacks a slow of up to 32%, reaching full stacks after 3.5s. When the channel ends, pigeons mob every breaded target for up to 450 spirit damage and hex them into a pigeon for up to 2.75s. More stacks mean more damage and a longer hex.

| Tier | Upgrade |
|---|---|
| 1 | +4m cast range and +32% max slow |
| 2 | -55s cooldown |
| 3 | +125 max damage, much higher spirit scaling and +0.5s hex |
